# Query
what was bengals CM's reponse after recent police encounter of state

# Response
West Bengal Chief Minister **Suvendu Adhikari defended the November 9 police encounter in Raiganj**, saying officers can return fire in self-defence. Police said Dilip Roy, an accused in the rape and murder of a minor, fired at officers when they tried to arrest him. Speaking in Digha, Adhikari said his government had given police a “free hand” to act without waiting for clearance from superiors in cases involving rape and murder, drug trafficking, and infiltration from Bangladesh. He said the government had zero tolerance for those offences.
