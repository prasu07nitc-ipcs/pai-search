# Query
karantak CM's opinion on e20 petrol

# Response
Karnataka Chief Minister D. K. Shivakumar said raising the ethanol content in petrol just to profit from cheaper ethanol was wrong. He urged the Centre to address the complaints and prioritize vehicle performance, fuel quality and public safety. His remarks on November 8 addressed complaints of excessive blending. The local report gives no explicit position from him on the standard 20% blend. [D. K. Shivakumar](data/index/www_thehindu_com/d__k__shivakumar/693c1e33fef8298cf60457ef9dd6176dda60ae9b50f9d65489743856205f0adc.md)

Shivakumar said Karnataka tested petrol at 67 locations after motorists reported problems including reduced mileage and engines stalling. He cited complaints of a 31.5% blend. The figure he cited came from complaints. [Karnataka](data/index/www_thehindu_com/karnataka/693c1e33fef8298cf60457ef9dd6176dda60ae9b50f9d65489743856205f0adc.md), [D. K.
