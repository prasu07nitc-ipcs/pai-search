# Query
ethanol mixing in petrol reports

# Response
**Karnataka.** The Hindu reported on November 9 that Karnataka tested petrol from 67 locations after motorists complained of reduced mileage and engine stalling. Chief Minister D. K. Shivakumar cited complaints of 31.5% ethanol. A separate report said some Karnataka samples tested as high as 31.2%, above the prescribed 20% level. **Keralam.** The state began random fuel checks in Thiruvananthapuram, Ernakulam, Kozhikode, and Kannur after the Karnataka findings and consumer complaints. Results were expected within 10 days of the November 9 announcement. The reports document complaints and excess ethanol in some Karnataka samples. They do not establish a nationwide rate or whether the blend caused the reported vehicle problems.
