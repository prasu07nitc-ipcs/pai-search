# [SIT to investigate Purvanchal Expressway collision; Rahul Gandhi unharmed](https://theprint.in/india/rahul-gandhis-convoy-involved-in-minor-collision-on-purvanchal-expressway-cong-leader-safe/3081514/)
*Updated: 2026-10-26T06:16:03+00:00*

A police investigation has been ordered into the collision involving vehicles accompanying Rahul Gandhi’s convoy in Sultanpur on October 26. Gandhi was unharmed, while a subsequent newspaper report put the number of people with minor injuries at five.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://staticprintenglish.theprint.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/theprint_default_image_new.jpg)

## Police widen inquiry into convoy collision

Uttar Pradesh Police formed a three-member Special Investigation Team to establish how the accident happened and examine possible failures in security, escort arrangements and traffic management. According to a police statement reported by [PTI/The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/uttar-pradesh/up-police-constitute-sit-to-probe-accident-involving-vehicles-in-rahul-gandhis-convoy/), the team must submit its findings to police headquarters and recommend preventive measures.

Lucknow Range Inspector General Kiran S heads the inquiry, joined by Sultanpur Additional Superintendent of Police Brij Narayan Singh and traffic Deputy Superintendent Ram Krishna Chaturvedi. The investigation was ordered by Director General of Police Rajeev Krishna. Its mandate describes issues to investigate, rather than established findings of negligence.

## Gandhi’s vehicle was not involved

The [original PTI report carried by ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/rahul-gandhis-convoy-involved-in-minor-collision-on-purvanchal-expressway-cong-leader-safe/3081514/) quoted police as saying vehicles collided after a private car braked suddenly for an unspecified reason. Congress spokesperson Anshu Awasthi said Gandhi was safe and his vehicle had not been involved. The convoy resumed its journey to Sultanpur for his court appearance.

Those initial police comments said no major injuries had been reported. [The Times of India’s October 27 account](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/five-injured-as-vehicles-behind-rahuls-convoy-collide-on-eway/articleshow/135218565.cms) subsequently reported five minor injuries, with treatment provided in an ambulance. It described at least five vehicles and an ambulance travelling behind the convoy as involved in the collision.

## Preliminary accounts leave questions open

The reports agree that Gandhi was not hurt, but their descriptions of the precise timing and sequence differ. The inquiry will examine those circumstances. The inspected coverage contains no completed SIT findings, so it does not establish final responsibility for the crash.
