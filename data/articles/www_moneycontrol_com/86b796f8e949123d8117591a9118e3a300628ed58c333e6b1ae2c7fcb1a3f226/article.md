# [Lava confirms Sunil Raina and Rajesh Sethi departures as it prepares new management structure](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/lava-md-sunil-raina-cfo-rajesh-sethi-quit-as-homegrown-smartphone-maker-seeks-turnaround-article-14058347.html)
*Updated: 2026-10-23T17:03:13+05:30*

Lava has confirmed that managing director Sunil Raina and chief financial officer Rajesh Sethi are leaving. The handset maker says it will announce its new management structure, while reports place co-founder Hari Om Rai back at the helm.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://images.moneycontrol.com/static-mcnews/2026/10/20261023053804_Sunil-Raina.jpeg)

## Company confirmation follows initial reports

A Lava spokesperson confirmed both departures to ETTelecom, whose report was updated on October 24. The company acknowledged the executives’ contributions and said details of its management structure and allocation of responsibilities would follow. That statement advances the initial account, which relied on unnamed sources and said Lava had not responded. [ETTelecom](https://telecom.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/devices/hari-om-rai-returns-to-lava-md-sunil-raina-quits-amid-underperformance/135143928).

ETTelecom’s industry sources said Rai had resumed the managing director role earlier in October. The report said Raina and Sethi were working through notice periods and would leave by year-end; the succession detail was attributed to sources rather than included in the company’s quoted confirmation. [ETTelecom](https://telecom.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/devices/hari-om-rai-returns-to-lava-md-sunil-raina-quits-amid-underperformance/135143928).

## Transition coincides with a funding search

Moneycontrol’s October 23 report said both executives would remain until December and that Raina intended to pursue opportunities outside Lava. It also reported that manufacturing chief Sanjeev Agarwal had joined Micron in July. [Moneycontrol’s original report](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/lava-md-sunil-raina-cfo-rajesh-sethi-quit-as-homegrown-smartphone-maker-seeks-turnaround-article-14058347.html).

The changes accompany a search for expansion funding. Moneycontrol reported in September that Lava was discussing a ₹500–600 crore raise with investors and that promoters of Dixon Technologies and Amber Enterprises were considering personal investments. Its sources said a transaction might also restructure some debt. These were reported negotiations, without a completed investment or final terms established in that account. [Moneycontrol’s funding report](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/dixon-amber-promoters-eye-stakes-in-lava-mobiles-as-new-mobile-scheme-priorities-indian-brands-14019815.html).

## Affordable phones remain central

Moneycontrol described an opportunity for Lava below ₹20,000 as rising component costs push some Chinese manufacturers towards more expensive devices. Raina told the publication that Lava’s smartphone business had grown 60–70% during the year, despite an overall market contraction. That growth figure is his reported assessment. [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/lava-md-sunil-raina-cfo-rajesh-sethi-quit-as-homegrown-smartphone-maker-seeks-turnaround-article-14058347.html).
