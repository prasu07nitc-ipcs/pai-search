# [Fadnavis backs BMC’s discretion after Bombay HC asks it to restore Dia Mirza’s Diwali video](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/maharashtra/bombay-hc-asks-bmc-to-re-upload-dia-mirzas-firecracker-appeal-says-deeply-pained-by-controversy/article71659945.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-03T15:41:56+05:30*

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis defended the Mumbai civic body’s discretion over its social media posts after the Bombay High Court criticised the removal of Dia Mirza’s anti-pollution video and called for its restoration on November 3, 2026.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/news/national/nmob9/article71659995.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/dia%20mirza%20pti.png)

## Chief minister downplays the controversy

Fadnavis said it was for the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) to decide what appeared on its own account, according to [The Indian Express’s report updated at 4:50 p.m.](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/mumbai/dia-mirza-diwali-video-civic-body-bmc-fadnavis-ritu-tawde-10948895/). Asked about the court’s displeasure, he questioned whether it had issued strictures and suggested it may have made observations.

His comments represented his response to the controversy. They do not establish that the court withdrew its request or approved the removal. The inspected reports do not confirm that the BMC subsequently restored the clip.

## Court calls for the video to return

Earlier, Justices Girish Kulkarni and Neela Gokhale objected to political criticism of Mirza’s participation in an environmental awareness campaign. The bench asked the civic body to put her video back, emphasising public health, secularism and community solidarity, [Bar & Bench reported](https://www.barandbench.com/amp/story/news/bombay-high-court-slams-politicians-for-targeting-dia-mirza-over-her-crackerless-diwali-appeal-asks-bmc-to-re-upload-her-video-2).

Reporting on the hearing used differing formulations: Bar & Bench described a direction to re-upload the clip, while [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/mumbai/bombay-high-court-disturbed-mumbai-civic-body-bmc-dia-mirza-pollution-free-diwali-video-10948459/) described the court suggesting that restoration would be appropriate. Both accounts establish that the bench wanted the video returned and disapproved of the controversy.

## An awareness campaign becomes a political dispute

The BMC removed Mirza’s video after objections from Mumbai BJP chief Ameet Satam and Mayor Ritu Tawde. Other celebrity messages remained available, according to the inspected reports. The campaign followed an October 26 court initiative encouraging celebrity participation in public education about firecracker pollution, [The Indian Express reported](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/mumbai/bombay-high-court-disturbed-mumbai-civic-body-bmc-dia-mirza-pollution-free-diwali-video-10948459/).

The bench rejected attempts to attach a communal meaning to the campaign. It also stressed that its anti-pollution directions applied throughout the year, rather than to Diwali alone, according to [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/amp/story/news/bombay-high-court-slams-politicians-for-targeting-dia-mirza-over-her-crackerless-diwali-appeal-asks-bmc-to-re-upload-her-video-2). The central issue before it remained the effect of firecrackers on noise and air pollution.
