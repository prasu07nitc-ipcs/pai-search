# [Madras High Court orders medical checks for four Theni jailbreak prisoners after assault allegations](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/theni-jail-warders-thrashed-17-escapees-after-recapture-alleges-mother-of-prisoners-at-madras-high-court/article71660084.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-03T15:29:54+05:30*

Four prisoners now held in Salem are to undergo a court-directed medical examination following allegations that warders assaulted inmates after the Theni jailbreak. The order comes alongside a wider legal-services inquiry into their treatment.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/incoming/405kzj/article71660360.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/THENIJAILBREAK_2.JPG)

## Medical team asked to report by November 6

The Madras High Court on November 3 directed three government doctors to examine four recaptured inmates at Salem Central Prison on November 4, provide necessary treatment and report by November 6, according to [The Hindu’s report](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/theni-jail-warders-thrashed-17-escapees-after-recapture-alleges-mother-of-prisoners-at-madras-high-court/article71660084.ece). Justices A.D. Jagadish Chandira and B. Karthikeyan issued the interim directions on a petition by K. Arumugathammal, mother of two of the prisoners.

The examination covers her sons, K. Meenakshisundaram and K. Tirupathi, and inmates Solai Mugileswaran and Sabarinath. The doctors are to accompany the Salem district legal services authority chairperson. Arumugathammal alleges that warders beat all 17 recaptured escapees on October 29 and that the four sent to Salem lacked proper treatment. These are allegations; the reported interim order does not establish that the assaults occurred. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/theni-jail-warders-thrashed-17-escapees-after-recapture-alleges-mother-of-prisoners-at-madras-high-court/article71660084.ece)

## Inquiry extends beyond the four Salem inmates

A separate inquiry ordered by the Tamil Nadu State Legal Services Authority addresses complaints about conditions before the escape and treatment afterward. Its Theni district wing was instructed to investigate allegations involving inadequate food, drinking water and other ill-treatment, including whether prisoners were deterred from complaining, [The Times of India reported on November 2](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/tnslsa-orders-inquiry-into-cause-of-theni-jailbreak/articleshow/135463989.cms).

Legal services authorities in Cuddalore, Madurai, Salem and Tirunelveli were also directed to question the transferred prisoners without prison officials present. Reports are due by November 16, according to the same report. That deadline concerns the broader inquiry, distinct from the November 6 medical-report deadline in the High Court proceedings.

## Police response and the escape timeline

Responding to the family’s allegations before the court hearing, Southern Zone Inspector General Vijendra Bidari said the apprehended inmates had undergone medical examinations before being produced before a magistrate and that the process was recorded on video. [The New Indian Express reported his response on November 2](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2026/Nov/02/21-escaped-from-tns-theni-jail-says-prisoner-in-video-shot-before-his-surrender-ig-dismisses-claim). That account does not resolve the subsequent assault allegations.

The 17 undertrial prisoners escaped from the district jail at Thekkampatti on October 26 and were all back in custody by October 28. A [PTI dispatch carried by ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/all-17-escaped-inmates-of-theni-jail-recaptured-within-43-hours/3084428/) described a search involving police teams and drones. Accounts differ on whether the last two brothers surrendered or were arrested; the established point across the reports is that the escape ended with all 17 in custody.

## Separate review of prison security

Tamil Nadu has also formed a committee led by former Director General of Police J.K. Tripathy to investigate security failures and recommend changes across the prison system. Its remit includes staffing, operating procedures, infrastructure and technology, according to [The Times of India’s November 2 report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/theni-jailbreak-tamil-nadu-govt-forms-three-member-panel-to-overhaul-prison-security/articleshow/135456178.cms).

The security review and the inquiries into prisoner treatment address different questions arising from the same escape. None of the inspected reports provides the results of the medical examination ordered for November 4.
