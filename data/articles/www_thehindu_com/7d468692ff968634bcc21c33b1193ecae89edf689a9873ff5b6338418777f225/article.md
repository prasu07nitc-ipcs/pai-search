# [Mahadevapura voter-roll report flags 803 IDs associated with 397 people after revision](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/in-karnataka-seat-where-vote-chori-claims-first-surfaced-over-800-voter-ids-linked-to-397-names-after-sir/article71661304.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-04T07:52:39+05:30*

A November 4 report by The Hindu raises fresh questions about duplicate registrations following Karnataka’s electoral-roll revision; the state’s election office says flagged cases will be checked.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/incoming/ue0h77/article71663719.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/Form-7-objectioG6VGL40RS.3.jpg.jpg)

## Findings awaiting verification

[The Hindu reports](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/in-karnataka-seat-where-vote-chori-claims-first-surfaced-over-800-voter-ids-linked-to-397-names-after-sir/article71661304.ece) that 803 voter IDs were associated with 397 people in Mahadevapura after the Special Intensive Revision (SIR). Its account describes both repeated EPIC numbers across constituencies and multiple IDs associated with matching personal details. Karnataka’s Chief Electoral Officer’s office reportedly said no formal duplicate-entry complaints had been received, while cases raised publicly would be verified.

The constituency featured in Rahul Gandhi’s earlier allegations of electoral manipulation. Those allegations and the newly reported roll discrepancies do not establish that duplicate votes were cast or an election result was altered.

The original webpage could not be accessed during this review; its findings and official response are attributed to the supplied article. No later verified resolution of these specific cases was located.

## Revision completed before the report

[The Times of India reported on October 27](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/sir-final-list-for-karnataka-is-out-check-your-name-download-electoral-roll-pdf-and-find-updated-serial-number/articleshow/135228997.cms) that Karnataka’s final SIR roll had been published after a 129-day exercise. Its account said voters could download electoral-roll PDFs and check updated details through election authorities’ services. This supports the timing of the final-roll publication, but does not independently corroborate The Hindu’s count.

## An ID-number collision needs a different check

In a [March 2, 2025 clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2107501&lang=2&reg=48), the Election Commission explained that identical EPIC numbers had sometimes been assigned to different people across states through older administrative systems. It said an ID-number match alone did not establish a fictitious or duplicate voter, and announced unique-number corrections.

That explanation concerned different electors sharing a number. It does not determine whether the matching personal records described in Mahadevapura represent duplicate registrations. Verification of those entries remains separate from any finding about votes actually cast.
