# [Lula wins fourth presidential term after reversing first-round deficit against Flávio Bolsonaro](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/brazils-lula-da-silva-defies-odds-to-clinch-poll-victory-against-flavio-bolsonaro/article71625437.ece)
*Updated: 2026-10-26T07:22:24+05:30*

Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has won Brazil’s presidential runoff, securing a fourth term after finishing behind Senator Flávio Bolsonaro in the first round. The victory leaves him facing a divided electorate and difficult economic and security demands.

## A reversal between rounds

Brazil’s Senate news agency reported that the electoral court considered Lula reelected at 21:08 on October 25, with 97.69% of counting completed. At that stage, he held 51.74% of valid votes against Bolsonaro’s 48.26%. Those figures describe a count in progress, rather than a final certified tally. [Agência Senado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2026/10/25/lula-e-reeleito-para-quarto-mandato-de-presidente).

The turnaround followed a first round in which Bolsonaro received 47.03% and Lula 45.16%, according to the Superior Electoral Court’s account with 99.99% of ballots counted. The court identifies Bolsonaro as a Rio de Janeiro senator and Lula as the incumbent serving his third term, having previously governed from 2003 to 2010. [Superior Electoral Court](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/flavio-bolsonaro-e-lula-vao-disputar-o-2o-turno-para-a-presidencia-da-republica).

## Voting extension and the next term

Senate reporting says Supreme Court Justice Cristiano Zanin extended voting until 19:00 following the Lula campaign’s complaints about transport access. The measure applied where polling remained open; closed ballot boxes were not reopened. Bolsonaro’s campaign also sought extra voting time because of rain in two states. [Agência Senado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2026/10/25/lula-e-reeleito-para-quarto-mandato-de-presidente).

The Hindu’s supplied account describes a campaign in which Lula mobilised rallies and changed his social media approach after the first-round setback. It reports that cost-of-living concerns and insecurity continued to trouble voters despite improvements in inflation, employment and hunger. It also identifies a hostile, right-dominated Congress and slower expected growth as constraints on his next administration. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/brazils-lula-da-silva-defies-odds-to-clinch-poll-victory-against-flavio-bolsonaro/article71625437.ece).
