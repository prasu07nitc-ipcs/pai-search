# [Gujarat search joins hunt for driver after Madhya Pradesh inspector’s death](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/madhya-pradesh/cop-killed-in-hit-and-run-mp-police-widen-probe-to-gujarat-in-search-of-absconding-driver/article71660735.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-03T17:49:14+05:30*

Madhya Pradesh police have extended their search into Gujarat for the truck driver accused of fatally running over Khategaon station house officer Dilip Puri, as investigators trace an alleged liquor-smuggling operation across several districts.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://www.thehindu.com/theme/images/og-image.png)

## Search spreads across the state border

A police team has gone to Gujarat because investigators suspect Gajendra alias Akku Rawat may be hiding there. Teams are also searching Alirajpur, Dhar, Jhabua, Dewas and Indore, places where he previously lived, according to the November 3 [PTI report carried by Rediff](https://www.rediff.com/news/report/mp-cop-killed-in-hit-and-run-police-probe-widens-to-gujarat/20261103.htm).

The same report says police have arrested Rahul Jaiswal, Keram Singh Changod and Vijay Rao, and announced a ₹10,000 reward for the arrest of each absconding accused. It describes officers examining Rawat’s relatives and contacts while seeking evidence of the wider network. This PTI account overlaps with the supplied original report and is not independent corroboration of every allegation.

## A highway interception turns fatal

Puri was killed early on November 2 on the Indore–Betul highway in Dewas district. Constable Sohan Jat was also injured. According to [The Times of India’s reporting](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/indore/inspector-45-run-over-by-truck-carrying-illicit-liquor-in-mps-dewas/articleshow/135472343.cms), the team approached a truck near Sandalpur Fata after receiving information about suspected liquor transport. Police alleged that the driver tried to escape over the divider and a rear wheel struck Puri.

The truck was subsequently abandoned. Police and excise officials found a concealed compartment containing about 1,800 litres of liquor, the newspaper reported, citing Dewas Superintendent of Police Aditya Mishra. Its November 3 account also identified Rawat as still absconding, consistent with the later PTI report.

## Investigators trace the liquor supply

Police allege that liquor from licensed shops in Harda district was being diverted to Gujarat through an interstate network. [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/madhya-pradesh/2026/Nov/02/mp-cop-crushed-to-death-by-truck-carrying-illicit-liquor-probe-uncovers-inter-state-smuggling-racket) reported that investigators accused Jaiswal of directing the operation and Keram Singh of supplying the truck.

Jaiswal denied involvement in the liquor business in a social-media statement before being taken into custody, saying he operated buses and a petrol pump, according to that report. The accusations against the arrested men remain police allegations.

The investigation has also prompted administrative action. An assistant district excise officer and two constables have been suspended, according to the [November 3 PTI account](https://www.rediff.com/news/report/mp-cop-killed-in-hit-and-run-police-probe-widens-to-gujarat/20261103.htm). The Times of India separately reported a high-level inquiry and a ₹1 crore assistance announcement for Puri’s family, alongside a compassionate appointment for his wife as a sub-inspector.
