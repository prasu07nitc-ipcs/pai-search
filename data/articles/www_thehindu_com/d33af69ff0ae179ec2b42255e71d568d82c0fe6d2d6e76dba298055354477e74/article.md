# [India says 14 nationals safe in Yemen as officials explore assistance and possible return](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/indian-embassy-seeks-facts-on-october-yacht-incident-off-yemen/article71614503.ece)
*Updated: 2026-10-23T16:27:35+05:30*

India says all 14 nationals involved in a vessel incident off Yemen are safe in Hodeidah. Officials are seeking contact through local parties and community members, while responsibility for the reported attack remains unconfirmed.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/news/national/f2ntt0/article71614577.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/iStock-1493034941.jpg)

## Ministry discusses assistance and possible return

At his October 23 briefing, Ministry of External Affairs spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said India understood that the 14 nationals were safe in Hodeidah. He said officials would work through local parties and community contacts to reach them, assess their welfare and consider their possible return to India. He described their vessel as a dhow, whereas earlier reports called it a yacht. The reported remarks did not establish a return date. [Rediff’s briefing report](https://m.rediff.com/amp/news/commentary/2026/oct/23/14-indian-onboard-dhow-off-yemen-coast-are-safe-mea/7e10593894e72c8e946d9ad8cf01cd84).

Earlier that day, the Indian embassy in Yemen said reports placed all 14 nationals safely around Hodeidah and that it would continue working for their welfare. The update followed its initial efforts to establish the circumstances of the incident. [Reuters, published by NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/14-indians-aboard-yacht-attacked-off-yemen-coast-safe-confirms-embassy-12224588).

## Attack allegation remains attributed to the Houthis

The Houthis said on October 22 that they were rescuing 14 Indians from a boat attacked near Jabal Zuqar island, close to the Bab el-Mandeb strait, and accused Saudi Arabia. Reuters reported that Saudi authorities had not answered its request for comment that Thursday. These accounts establish that the allegation was made; they do not independently establish who attacked the vessel. [Reuters report](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/14-indians-aboard-yacht-attacked-off-yemen-coast-safe-confirms-embassy-12224588).

The supplied account from The Hindu likewise said the Indian embassy had not specifically attributed the attack. Its central development was the safety update, following earlier uncertainty over the nationals’ circumstances. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/indian-embassy-seeks-facts-on-october-yacht-incident-off-yemen/article71614503.ece).
