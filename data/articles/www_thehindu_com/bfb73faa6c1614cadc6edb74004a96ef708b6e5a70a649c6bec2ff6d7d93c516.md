## Title

P.T. Usha complains to IOA electoral officer over WhatsApp campaign for SOM polls

## Description

IOA president P.T. Usha complained that Dola Banerjee violated the Model Code of Conduct with a WhatsApp message seeking votes in the SOM election.

## Body

Indian Olympic Association (IOA) president P.T. Usha has lodged a complaint with the Electoral Officer alleging that former archer Dola Banerjee violated the Model Code of Conduct by circulating a WhatsApp message soliciting votes for a panel of four candidates in Monday’s (November 9, 2026) Sportspersons of Outstanding Merit (SOM) elections.

In her complaint, Usha objected to a WhatsApp message circulated through an account bearing Banerjee’s name, seeking support for the ‘New Bharat Panel’ and asking voters to elect four named candidates, along with their ballot serial numbers and roll numbers.

Usha cited Clause 1.9 of the Model Code of Conduct, issued on October 30, 2026, which states that the promotion of a candidate shall exclude any form of publicity, including the use of new media or social networks. She contended that the circulation of the message amounted to targeted solicitation of votes through social media, and appeared to fall within the ambit of the clause. The complaint also referred to Clauses 1.2 and 1.3, which require candidates to conduct their campaigns fairly, with dignity and moderation, and to show due respect to other candidates.

Usha has urged the election authorities to examine the communication, establish the circumstances under which it was created and circulated, and seek an explanation from the persons concerned. She has also sought appropriate action under the applicable election rules if a violation is established, along with directions to ensure a level playing field for all candidates.

With polling scheduled from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on November 9, 2026, Usha has sought urgent intervention and a decision before voting begins. A screenshot of the WhatsApp message and a copy of the Model Code of Conduct were enclosed with the complaint.
