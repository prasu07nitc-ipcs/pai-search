# [175 years after Ariel and Umbriel, Uranus still rewards the search for hidden moons](https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/science/ariel-umbriel-and-the-man-who-made-them-real/article71587499.ece)
*Updated: 2026-10-24T10:34:02+05:30*

Uranus has 29 recognized moons in JPL’s current catalogue. As the discovery of Ariel and Umbriel reaches its 175th anniversary, modern observations show how much the planet’s satellite system still has to reveal. [JPL’s discovery catalogue](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/discovery.html).

## From Lassell’s telescope to Webb

The latest addition listed by JPL is S/2025 U1, detected in James Webb Space Telescope observations made on February 2, 2025. NASA announced the finding that August. A team led by the Southwest Research Institute identified the faint object in long exposures from Webb’s near-infrared camera; its estimated diameter is about 10 kilometres, assuming reflectivity similar to nearby small moons. NASA initially described the work as science in progress that had not yet undergone peer review. [NASA’s discovery announcement](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/webb/2025/08/19/new-moon-discovered-orbiting-uranus-using-nasas-webb-telescope/).

That modern search gives fresh context to William Lassell’s achievement on October 24, 1851. NASA credits the English amateur astronomer with discovering both Ariel and Umbriel on that date. The anniversary marked in The Hindu’s October 24 account therefore spans 175 years of increasingly powerful observation. [NASA on Ariel](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/ariel/), [NASA on Umbriel](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/umbriel/), [The Hindu’s anniversary account](https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/science/ariel-umbriel-and-the-man-who-made-them-real/article71587499.ece).

## A brewer who built the means to discover

Lassell financed his astronomy with wealth from brewing. The supplied historical account describes his Starfield observatory in Liverpool and a 24-inch reflecting telescope whose mounting allowed sustained tracking of celestial objects. Instrument making was central to his work: detecting faint companions required both a good mirror and the ability to keep observing a target. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/science/ariel-umbriel-and-the-man-who-made-them-real/article71587499.ece).

His discoveries extended beyond Uranus. He found Neptune’s largest moon, Triton, on October 10, 1846, only 17 days after the planet’s discovery. In 1848 he independently found Saturn’s Hyperion; William Cranch Bond and George Phillips Bond also discovered it, and all three share credit. [NASA on Triton](https://science.nasa.gov/neptune/moons/triton/), [NASA on Hyperion](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/hyperion/).

## Literary names, contrasting worlds

Ariel’s name links astronomy with both Shakespeare’s *The Tempest* and Alexander Pope’s *The Rape of the Lock*. NASA records that John Herschel supplied the name at Lassell’s request. Umbriel takes its name from a spirit in Pope’s poem. [Ariel’s naming history](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/ariel/), [Umbriel’s naming history](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/umbriel/).

The two worlds are physically distinct. Ariel has the brightest surface among Uranus’s five largest moons and evidence of comparatively recent geological activity. Umbriel is the darkest of those moons, with an ancient cratered surface and a conspicuous bright ring seen by Voyager 2 in 1986. NASA says the ring’s origin remains uncertain. Their discovery established their existence; later observations began explaining what they are like. [NASA on Ariel](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/ariel/), [NASA on Umbriel](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/umbriel/).
