## Title

Sheikh Hasina's request for new passport being examined by Bangladesh Foreign Ministry

## Description

Hasina seeks a new passport to return from exile as Bangladesh pursues extradition, amid political turmoil and controversial verdicts.

## Body

Hours after a legal team representing deposed Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina sought a new passport for her, the Government of Bangladesh asserted on Monday (November 9, 2026) that Ms. Hasina can return to Bangladesh through an “acceptable prisoner exchange” programme governed by the India-Bangladesh extradition treaty.

Speaking to the media in Dhaka, the country’s Acting Foreign Minister Humayun Kabir described Ms. Hasina as a “terrorist” who had fled to India after committing crimes.

A source in Dhaka told *The Hindu* that Ms. Hasina has requested the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, through a London-based legal firm, for a new passport and a National Identity Card. The official said the Ministry is verifying the letter and will take a final decision after internal consultations. Ms. Hasina’s passport was revoked soon after she escaped to India in August 2024 after an uprising toppled her government.

“Bangladesh will not issue a passport to any terrorist; there is no provision for such an action. Bangladesh will seek the return of Sheikh Hasina through an acceptable prisoner exchange process,” said Mr. Kabir, in response to questions from journalists regarding the request.

He noted that Ms. Hasina is a convict sentenced to death and India should hand over the ousted Prime Minister under the extradition treaty. He added that Ms. Hasina and several of her colleagues from the Awami League are responsible for violent crimes and financial fraud, and need to be brought to justice.

The Ministry of External Affairs is yet to respond to Mr. Kabir’s remarks, but a source familiar with the Indian stance on the matter told *The Hindu* that the Bangladeshi position of not issuing a passport to Ms. Hasina will further complicate the matter, as a passport will be needed if India were to consider her extradition.

#### Firm sends email

The Bangladeshi official, who spoke to *The Hindu*, said over the phone, “We have received an email from Matrix Chambers, a law firm in London, seeking a new passport for Sheikh Hasina. We are currently examining the contents of the letter and will take an appropriate step after internal consultation.”

However, the Press Secretary of the Bangladesh High Commission, Md. Saidur Rahman, said the Bangladesh authorities are “not sure if the request is genuine”. “There is no way for us to verify whether the request was actually made on behalf of Sheikh Hasina, as the email sent by the law firm did not include any authorisation letter or supporting documents proving that the firm was authorised by her to make such a request,” he said.

India had earlier acknowledged receiving Bangladesh’s extradition requests for Ms. Hasina but has not announced any decision on handing her over. The External Affairs Ministry had said the requests are being examined as part of its established processes.

A number of Awami League leaders, who fled to other countries after the uprising, have said on social media that preparations were underway for Ms. Hasina’s return.
