# [Supreme Court asks Calcutta High Court to consider Abhishek Banerjee’s media-trial plea](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/supreme-court-grants-liberty-to-abhishek-banerjee-asks-calcutta-hc-to-consider-plea-against-media-trial/article71660680.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-03T18:09:13+05:30*

The Supreme Court has allowed Abhishek Banerjee to seek consideration of interim relief in the Calcutta High Court against alleged investigative leaks and media trials. The reported November 3 direction concerns a hearing of his request; it does not itself impose the reporting restrictions he seeks.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/incoming/vu2cws/article71660767.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/TH12-KDR--do-noGPVGLEUHS.3.jpg.jpg)

## Interim request returns to the High Court

A bench comprising Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana said the Trinamool Congress MP could bring the matter to the High Court’s attention when it reopens on November 16. The bench requested consideration of his interim application, according to [LiveLaw’s November 3 court report](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-asks-calcutta-high-court-to-consider-abhishek-banerjees-plea-against-media-reports-on-investigations-against-him-557088).

Senior advocate Shoeb Alam told the Supreme Court that the writ petition had been filed in September and encountered a roster issue. He said three subsequent mentions had not secured a hearing while reports continued to appear. Alam also said Banerjee sought takedown orders and argued that media trials undermined the presumption of innocence, LiveLaw reported.

The reported direction leaves the interim request for the High Court to consider. It should not be read as a finding that investigating agencies leaked information or that particular reporting violated Banerjee’s rights.

## What Banerjee alleges

According to [The Hindu’s supplied account](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/supreme-court-grants-liberty-to-abhishek-banerjee-asks-calcutta-hc-to-consider-plea-against-media-trial/article71660680.ece), Banerjee alleges that investigators selectively shared personal information and details of searches, questioning and other steps with news outlets. He contends that these disclosures damaged his reputation and could affect ongoing investigations.

His petition invokes dignity, reputation and a fair investigation under Article 21. The relief he seeks includes restraints on agencies sharing his information and on media reports concerning matters under investigation. These are his allegations and requested remedies, rather than findings recorded against the agencies or news organisations.

## Earlier efforts to secure a hearing

[LiveLaw’s October 13 report](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-declines-urgent-hearing-of-abhishek-banerjees-plea-against-media-trial-554393) said Justice Saugata Bhattacharya had declined an urgent hearing before the Puja vacation and placed the matter in the December monthly list. It also reported that, on October 8, Justice Krishna Rao had directed that the plea go before the bench handling police-action matters.

The Supreme Court’s latest intervention gives Banerjee an opportunity to seek earlier consideration after reopening. A decision granting or refusing the requested restraints is a separate step.
