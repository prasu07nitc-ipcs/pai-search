# [Indian Navy says 20 rescued from hijacked Azini in operation completed November 2](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/indian-navy-rescues-hijacked-fishing-vessel-off-somalia-frees-all-20-crew-members/article71659504.ece)
*Updated: 2026-11-03T12:10:05+05:30*

India’s Defence Ministry says a Navy operation lasting more than 48 hours freed the fishing vessel Azini and rescued 20 crew members without injuries, following its hijacking off northeastern Somalia.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/news/national/eov2mg/article71659516.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/IndianNavySomalia.jpg)

## Rescue completed before Tuesday’s announcement

The operation ended on November 2 in the Arabian Sea, more than 3,000 kilometres from Mumbai, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh said, according to [DD India’s November 3 report](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/11/indian-navy-rescues-hijacked-fishing-vessel-off-somalia-frees-all-20-crew-members/). Singh credited timely Indian Air Force support.

A [Defence Ministry statement published by the Press Information Bureau on November 3](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2331872&lang=2&reg=48) said all 20 crew members were rescued, with no casualties or injuries. The announcement establishes the government’s account of the outcome; the operation itself took place earlier.

## Aircraft tracked the vessel; commandos arrived by airdrop

The ministry said a deployed warship and maritime reconnaissance aircraft located Azini, monitored its movements and used non-kinetic measures to restrict its escape. An Air Force C-17 delivered additional Marine Commandos by airdrop before naval personnel secured the vessel.

The [official statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2331872&lang=2&reg=48) described concern that pirates could use the fishing vessel as a base for attacks on merchant ships. It did not establish that such further attacks had occurred or specify what subsequently happened to the pirates.

## Earlier headcount remains unexplained

Before the rescue announcement, [Associated Press reported on November 1](https://apnews.com/article/somalia-pirates-hijacking-iranian-fishing-vessel-dd4d404235781a3b871668c353540e4c) that Azini had been seized between Bandar Bayla and Hafun. Citing a Puntland maritime security officer, it counted 21 Iranian crew members and two Somali security guards aboard.

That earlier total of 23 differs from the ministry’s later account of 20 rescued crew members. The official release does not reconcile the figures or address the guards’ status. The rescue count here follows the ministry’s latest statement; the earlier report cannot establish the fate of any additional people.
