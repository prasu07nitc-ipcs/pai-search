# [BJP leader counters Thomson’s ticket claim after Venugopal demands CEC’s removal](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/former-chief-secretarys-revelations-against-cec-extremely-serious-kc-venugopal/article71513776.ece)
*Updated: 2026-09-27T00:03:01+05:30*

A BJP leader challenged former Kerala Chief Secretary Jiji Thomson’s account of a political approach by Gyanesh Kumar, after Congress leader K.C. Venugopal demanded the Chief Election Commissioner’s removal. The competing statements concern an alleged conversation from 2016; they do not establish electoral wrongdoing.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://th-i.thgim.com/public/incoming/o25j2y/article71514034.ece/alternates/LANDSCAPE_1200/TH17-Sobhana-PAGAPGHTB2R.16.jpg.jpg)

## BJP response follows Congress demand

BJP leader Jiji Joseph responded on September 27 by pointing to Thomson’s attendance at a Minority Morcha event in Kochi in June 2017. Joseph claimed that Thomson praised Narendra Modi and later served as an independent director of Cochin Shipyard between 2017 and 2020. He questioned Thomson’s motives; PTI reported that Thomson had not responded to Joseph’s allegations at the time of that report. These are Joseph’s assertions, rather than independent findings about Thomson’s intentions. [PTI, via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/bjp-leader-counters-thomsons-cec-ticket-claim-cites-2017-event-cochin-shipyard-appointment/3054825/).

The response followed Venugopal’s description of Thomson’s disclosure as “extremely serious.” The AICC general secretary alleged that Kumar’s appointment as CEC rewarded service to the BJP and urged his immediate removal to protect the Election Commission’s integrity. He also accused Kumar of undermining elections and said dissent was emerging within the NDA. Those charges were Venugopal’s political allegations. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/former-chief-secretarys-revelations-against-cec-extremely-serious-kc-venugopal/article71513776.ece).

## What Thomson alleged

Thomson told Onmanorama that Kumar, then an IAS officer, encouraged him during a flight to Delhi to contest the 2019 Lok Sabha election from Pathanamthitta as a BJP candidate. Thomson placed the conversation during his tenure as Kerala Chief Secretary and said he declined. He also recalled an earlier attempt by Kumar to persuade him to enter politics. His recollection is an attributed account of a private conversation. [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2026/09/26/gyanesh-kumar-offered-bjp-candidature-from-pathanamthitta-kerala-ex-cs-jiji-thomson.amp.html).

## Wider dispute over the poll panel

The controversy unfolded alongside reports of disagreements within the Election Commission over voter-registration procedures and electoral-roll data. The Commission separately maintained that its orders and administrative directions had legal sanction and that differing views formed part of normal institutional deliberations, according to The New Indian Express. That institutional response concerned the wider dispute, rather than a rebuttal of Thomson’s specific conversation claim. [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/kerala/2026/Sep/26/cec-gyanesh-kumar-asked-me-to-contest-2019-ls-polls-for-bjp-ex-kerala-chief-secretary-alleges).
