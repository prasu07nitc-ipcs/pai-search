# [Five minor injuries reported after collision behind Rahul Gandhi’s convoy; SIT examines arrangements](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/sit-formed-to-probe-accident-involving-vehicles-behind-rahul-gandhis-convoy-in-sultanpur/articleshow/135205862.cms)
*Updated: 2026-10-26T15:52:00+05:30*

Five people sustained minor injuries in a collision behind Rahul Gandhi’s convoy on October 26, according to subsequent reporting. Uttar Pradesh Police have appointed a three-member investigation team to establish what happened and whether convoy arrangements contributed.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-135206008,width-1280,height-720,resizemode-6,overlay-toi_sw,pt-32,y_pad-600/photo.jpg)

## Later account reports minor injuries

The Times of India reported on October 27 that at least five vehicles and an ambulance travelling behind the convoy collided on the Purvanchal Expressway in Sultanpur. It said five people received treatment for minor injuries inside the ambulance. The newspaper described an abrupt halt followed by vehicles crashing into one another. These details remain a reported account rather than the investigation team’s findings. [The Times of India, October 27](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/five-injured-as-vehicles-behind-rahuls-convoy-collide-on-eway/articleshow/135218565.cms).

An earlier PTI report quoted a police officer as saying there were no major injuries. Congress spokesperson Anshu Awasthi told PTI that Gandhi was safe and his own vehicle was not involved. PTI said the affected vehicles had joined the convoy along the route and carried passengers including Congress workers from Amethi. [PTI, via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/rahul-gandhis-convoy-involved-in-minor-collision-on-purvanchal-expressway-cong-leader-safe/3081514/).

## Inquiry into security, escort and traffic management

Director General of Police Rajeev Krishna ordered the inquiry. Lucknow Range Inspector General Kiran S heads the SIT, with Sultanpur Additional Superintendent of Police Brij Narayan Singh and Deputy Superintendent of Police for Traffic R.K. Chaturvedi as members. [The Times of India, original report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/sit-formed-to-probe-accident-involving-vehicles-behind-rahul-gandhis-convoy-in-sultanpur/articleshow/135205862.cms).

The team must reconstruct the collision and examine possible shortcomings in security, escort management and traffic regulation. It will also propose ways to prevent similar incidents during VIP movements and submit a detailed report to police headquarters as soon as possible. The announcement establishes the scope of the inquiry; it does not establish that negligence occurred. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/sit-formed-to-probe-accident-involving-vehicles-behind-rahul-gandhis-convoy-in-sultanpur/articleshow/135205862.cms).

Gandhi was travelling to Sultanpur for a defamation-case hearing. PTI reported that the journey continued after the collision. [PTI, via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/rahul-gandhis-convoy-involved-in-minor-collision-on-purvanchal-expressway-cong-leader-safe/3081514/).
