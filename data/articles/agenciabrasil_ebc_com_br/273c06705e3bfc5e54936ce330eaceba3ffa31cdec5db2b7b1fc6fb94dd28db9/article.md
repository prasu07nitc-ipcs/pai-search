# [Lula wins fourth term as Brazil’s election authority confirms runoff result](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/en/politica/noticia/2026-10/lula-re-elected-president-brazil-historic-comeback)
*Updated: 2026-10-26T12:08:00-03:00 (inferred)*

Brazil’s election authority confirmed Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva’s reelection after an October 25 runoff shaped by renewed voter mobilization. His defeated challenger, Flávio Bolsonaro, subsequently announced plans to organize opposition to the president.

![Image accompanying the original article](https://imagens.ebc.com.br/s2u9Xr5CuqsyArLKk5Gl3sVj39g=/1600x800/https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/sites/default/files/thumbnails/image/2026/10/25/_int0285.jpg.jpeg?itok=Zko9T9o9)

## Official count confirms Lula’s victory

The [Superior Electoral Court (TSE)](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/presidente-do-tse-anuncia-reeleicao-de-lula-e-faz-balanco-do-2o-turno-das-eleicoes-2026) reported 64,256,232 votes for Lula, or 51.79% of valid ballots. Bolsonaro received 59,826,041 votes, equivalent to 48.21%. TSE president Kassio Nunes Marques announced the result on election night.

The court said approximately 128.2 million people voted, with abstention around 19.2%. It also described operational difficulties caused by a court decision extending voting until 7 p.m. at stations still open. Some machines had already closed and could not be reopened, requiring adjustments to the processing of results.

## Mobilization behind the comeback

[Agência Brasil’s account](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/en/politica/noticia/2026-10/lula-re-elected-president-brazil-historic-comeback) attributes momentum in Lula’s runoff campaign to online organizing and demonstrations. It describes more informal videos addressed directly to voters and credits lawmaker André Janones with creating a WhatsApp mobilization network with more than 10 million participants. Students and social movements also organized street demonstrations in numerous cities, according to the report.

The result delivered a fourth presidential term for Lula. [Agência Câmara](https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/1313705-lula-e-reeleito-presidente-com-quase-52-dos-votos-validos-no-2o-turno) described it as the first presidential runoff reversal by a candidate who had finished second in the initial round.

## Opposition takes shape

[Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/brazil-election-lula-bolsonaro-annul-contested-voting-0ef0b7b949fea74ff119c3f81453416b) reported that Bolsonaro initially sought annulment but, in a Monday video, instead emphasized organizing opposition to Lula. AP also reported that Donald Trump questioned the result without providing evidence. Those allegations do not alter the official result announced by the TSE.
