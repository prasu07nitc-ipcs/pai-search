# Salem Central Prison

## Sequence: **10/17**
- Utterance: Salem Central Prison received K. Meenakshisundaram, K. Tirupathi, Solai Mugileswaran and Sabarinath after the recaptured Theni escapees were transferred across Tamil Nadu. K. Arumugathammal claimed the four were sent there without proper treatment for injuries from severe warder beatings. On November 3 the Madras High Court ordered three government doctors to visit the prison with the Salem district legal services authority chairperson on November 4, examine the four, provide necessary treatment, and report by November 6.
