# Lava International

## Sequence: **1/17**
- Utterance: Lava International managing director Sunil Raina and chief financial officer Rajesh Sethi have quit, sources told Moneycontrol, as the homegrown handset maker seeks fresh capital and growth opportunities to revive its business. Co-founder Hari Om Rai, who stepped down as managing director in 2023, has returned to the role. Raina and Sethi are serving notice periods and will remain until December, the sources said. Chief manufacturing officer Sanjeev Agarwal had left to join Micron in July. Lava did not respond to Moneycontrol’s queries. Lava is seeking opportunities in smartphones priced below Rs 20,000 and is looking to raise capital for expansion. Promoters of Dixon Technologies and Amber Enterprises are discussing acquiring stakes in their personal capacities. Moneycontrol earlier reported separate talks with several investors to raise around Rs 500-600 crore for expansion and potentially restructuring part of its debt. A capital infusion could strengthen its smartphone portfolio, expand manufacturing and improve competitiveness. Lava is expected to apply for the Centre’s Rs 62,500-crore Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme, notified in August. It recently won half of Uttar Pradesh’s tender for 25 lakh tablets, with Acer winning the other half. Raina told Moneycontrol at the India Mobile Congress earlier this month that Lava’s smartphone business grew 60-70 percent this year while the overall market contracted, and that it focused on taking market share from competitors rather than relying on industry growth.
