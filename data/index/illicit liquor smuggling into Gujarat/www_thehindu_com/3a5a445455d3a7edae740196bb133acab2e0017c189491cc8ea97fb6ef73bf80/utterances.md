# illicit liquor smuggling into Gujarat

## Sequence: **13/13**
- Utterance: Officials said attempts to smuggle illicit liquor into Gujarat, described as a dry state, through border districts of western Madhya Pradesh continued to emerge. They said smugglers build special secret compartments in trucks, containers and tankers, and liquor cartons are often concealed in consignments of animal feed, grains, cement, vegetables or other goods to evade law enforcement agencies. Officials said illicit liquor consignments are smuggled through unpaved, rural and interior routes when main highways have heavy security.
