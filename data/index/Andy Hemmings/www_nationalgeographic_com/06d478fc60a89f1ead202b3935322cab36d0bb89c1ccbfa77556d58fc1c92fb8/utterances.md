# Andy Hemmings

## Sequence: **16/27**
- Utterance: Aucilla Research Institute archaeologist Andy Hemmings compares ancient river locations with water, animals and toolstone to a corner with Walmart and Home Depot, illustrating their possible appeal to hunter-gatherers. In 2009 he followed the Suwannee River channel 125 miles offshore from northern Florida to a site his team called Thor's Elbow. He dove 130 feet onto an ancient riverbank, imagining nobody had stood there in 11,000 years. Limited to nine minutes at that depth, he left equipment for a return coring visit, but a tropical storm prevented it and no subsequent expedition received funding. The potential deepwater sites remain inaccessible; he hopes divers and underwater drones will eventually study them thoroughly.
