# Article 324 (5) of the Constitution

## Sequence: **6/11**
- Utterance: Article 324 (5) of the Constitution deals with removal of the Chief Election Commissioner in a similar manner and on similar grounds as removal of a Supreme Court judge. The article contrasts this provision with Article 61, under which the term impeachment is reserved for the President’s office.
