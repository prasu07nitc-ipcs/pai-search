# Dilip Puri

## Sequence: **3/13**
- Utterance: Khategaon Station House Officer Dilip Puri was killed while trying to intercept a truck carrying 1,800 litres of illicit liquor on the Indore-Betul Highway on Monday, November 2, 2026; a constable was injured. Puri had gone to the highway after receiving information about illicit liquor smuggling and tried to question the driver of a truck parked near a roadside eatery. The driver allegedly drove over the road divider to escape, and the truck’s rear wheel ran over Puri. Dewas Superintendent of Police Aditya Mishra said efforts were being made to gather as much scientific evidence as possible in the case of the police station in-charge crushed to death by a truck carrying over 1,800 litres of illicit liquor.
