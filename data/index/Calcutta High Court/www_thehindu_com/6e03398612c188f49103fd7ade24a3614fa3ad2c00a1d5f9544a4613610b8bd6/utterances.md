# Calcutta High Court

## Sequence: **3/11**
- Utterance: On November 3, 2026, the Supreme Court asked the Calcutta High Court to consider Lok Sabha MP Abhishek Banerjee’s plea against media trial related to investigations and cases against him. According to Banerjee’s senior advocate Shoeb Alam, the High Court had taken up the matter only once, when the petitioner was told it did not have the roster; Alam said he had mentioned it three times since, but the petition had not been heard. The Supreme Court noted that the High Court would reopen on November 16 after the Durga Puja and Diwali break and said Banerjee could mention the matter for consideration of interim relief then.
