# gross GST collection

## Sequence: **5/6**
- Utterance: According to Minister D. Logesh Tamilselvan, gross GST collection includes SGST, the Union government’s CGST and IGST. He said the whole IGST collected cannot be considered Tamil Nadu’s revenue because some amounts are attributable to other States; Tamil Nadu’s actual GST revenue consists of SGST collections and its apportioned IGST settlement. He argued that gross GST collection figures alone could not measure Tamil Nadu’s economic performance, particularly after September 2025 GST rate rationalisation reduced rates on several commodities.
