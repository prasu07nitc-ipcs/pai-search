# 17 escaped prisoners

## Sequence: **2/17**
- Utterance: Seventeen prisoners escaped Theni district jail on October 26, 2026 and were recaptured within 42 hours. K. Arumugathammal alleged all 17 were beaten black and blue by prison warders after recapture, specifically with lathis and boots at Theni district jail on October 29. She attributed the escape of her sons and 15 other inmates to alleged ill-treatment. The recaptured prisoners were subsequently transferred to different prisons across Tamil Nadu. The Tamil Nadu State Legal Services Authority directed district legal services authority chairpersons on Monday to visit the prisons currently holding all 17 and inquire with them.
