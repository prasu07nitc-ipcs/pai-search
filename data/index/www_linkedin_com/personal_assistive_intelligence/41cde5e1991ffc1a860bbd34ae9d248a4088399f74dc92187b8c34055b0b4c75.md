# Personal Assistive Intelligence

## Sequence: **1/18**
- Utterance: PAI is an architecture that combines general-purpose AI models, goal-directed agents, operational harnesses and evolving personal context to extend an individual’s ability to reason, learn, decide and act. It carries relevant understanding across interactions and incorporates knowledge, preferences, objectives, constraints and decision principles into interpretation and action. Its personal core establishes relevance, portability preserves relevance across boundaries, and networkability brings in other people’s capabilities and experience. The individual contributes intent, expertise, experience and judgment, models and agents contribute reasoning, synthesis, planning and action, and the harness connects these contributions through memory, tools, orchestration and oversight. Transferred context needs personal interpretation, shared expertise needs adaptation, and both rely on managed permissions, execution and feedback. Success is measured by whether the architecture helps the individual understand situations, make decisions and accomplish meaningful goals while retaining judgment and control. It requires meaningful memory, selective transfer, trustworthy exchange and clear user control to develop with the individual over time.
