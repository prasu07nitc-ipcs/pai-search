# Personal context

## Sequence: **6/18**
- Utterance: Personal context includes domain knowledge, accumulated experience, preferences, priorities, objectives, constraints and definitions of success that affect alternatives, trade-offs and acceptable outcomes. It shapes recommendations and actions, with actions respecting the user’s authority boundaries. Capture, retrieval and application can improve personalization without retraining the model. Repeated instructions establish objectives and constraints, corrections expose mismatches with intent, and explanations reveal decision reasoning. Recording a vendor choice captures an event, while recording why reliability outweighed price captures a reusable principle whose scope matters: reliability may dominate critical purchases, while price can matter more for routine ones. Context should preserve rationale and applicability and remain inspectable, correctable and under user control as preferences, circumstances and interpretations change.
