# Personal context

## Sequence: **3/18**
- Utterance: Personal context encompasses the individual’s knowledge, domain expertise, experience, preferences, objectives, priorities, constraints, decision principles, and definitions of success. These shape which options the person considers, how they evaluate trade-offs, and which outcomes they accept. Recommendations should reflect the individual’s objectives and judgment, and actions should respect their constraints and authority boundaries. Context capture, retrieval, and application depend on identifying meaningful information, when it remains valid, and where it applies. Repeated interactions build personal context through instructions that establish objectives, preferences, constraints, and expectations, corrections that reveal mismatches with intent, and explanations that expose rationale and trade-offs. Recording a vendor decision captures an event, whereas recording why reliability outweighed price captures a potentially reusable principle. Reliability may outweigh price for critical supplies, while price may matter more for routine purchases. Context should preserve preferences and their conditions, and remain inspectable, correctable, and under user control as preferences and circumstances change or prior interpretations prove wrong.
