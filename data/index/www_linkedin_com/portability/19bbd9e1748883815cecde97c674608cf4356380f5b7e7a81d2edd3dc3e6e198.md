# Portability

## Sequence: **4/16**
- Utterance: Portability carries accumulated knowledge, preferences, methods, and decision principles across tasks, domains, tools, and platforms. Across domains it brings relevant judgment to unfamiliar problems. Across platforms it preserves continuity when changing models, applications, or providers. A hypothetical user’s vendor evaluations teach an agent to require verifiable evidence for material claims, favour long-term reliability over short-term savings, assess total ownership costs, and make material risks, assumptions, and trade-offs explicit. Those principles can inform cloud-provider evaluation, with different evidence such as service-level commitments, security controls, scalability, migration costs, and long-term dependency. Transfer must be selective and distinguish enduring judgment from local habits and situational exceptions. A new platform must be able to understand and use transferred context. Structured exports, sufficiently consistent interpretation across systems, and preservation of scope, rationale, and conditions make accumulated context durable as technology changes.
