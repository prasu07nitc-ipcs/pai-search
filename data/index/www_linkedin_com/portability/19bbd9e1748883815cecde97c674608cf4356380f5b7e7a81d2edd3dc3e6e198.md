# Portability

## Sequence: **4/18**
- Utterance: Portability carries an individual’s accumulated knowledge, preferences, methods, judgment, and decision principles across tasks, tools, domains, and platforms. Across domains, relevant judgment can inform unfamiliar problems. Across platforms, portability preserves continuity when users change models, applications, or providers. Transfer must be selective, assessing each preference’s relevance and distinguishing enduring judgment from local habits and situational exceptions. Cross-platform continuity requires new systems to understand and apply transferred context beyond synchronising conversation archives. It relies on context portability, semantic interoperability, and contextual adaptability. Accumulated context can remain a durable resource for the individual as technology changes.
