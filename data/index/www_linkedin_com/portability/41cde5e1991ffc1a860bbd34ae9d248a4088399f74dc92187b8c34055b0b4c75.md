# Portability

## Sequence: **7/18**
- Utterance: Portability carries accumulated knowledge, preferences, methods and judgment across tasks, tools, domains and platforms. Across domains it informs unfamiliar problems, and across platforms it preserves continuity when changing models, applications or providers. Transfer is selective because enduring principles, local habits and situational exceptions have different scopes. Cross-platform portability requires the receiving system to interpret and apply the context, including the rationale, evidence and exceptions behind preferences. This lets personal context retain value as technology changes.
