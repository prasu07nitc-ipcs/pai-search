# Permission and privacy

## Sequence: **16/18**
- Utterance: Trusted exchange requires explicit authorization for what can be shared, with whom and for what purpose. Reusable methods stay separate from private execution data, so sharing a procurement method should avoid exposing contracts, pricing and priorities. A shared workflow’s access and authority remain subject to the individual’s controls.
