# Memory

## Sequence: **9/18**
- Utterance: Memory provides continuity by preserving relevant knowledge, preferences, experience, and judgment. Its usefulness depends on retrieving and applying appropriate information more than on the volume stored. The harness can make that information available for reasoning and execution. Effective memory also requires revision as preferences and circumstances change and earlier interpretations prove incorrect. Personal context should remain inspectable, correctable, and controlled by the user.
