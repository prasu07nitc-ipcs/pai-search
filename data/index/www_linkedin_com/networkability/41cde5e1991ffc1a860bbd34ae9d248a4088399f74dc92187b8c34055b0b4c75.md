# Networkability

## Sequence: **8/18**
- Utterance: Networkability connects an individual to people, agents, knowledge sources and reusable workflows. Since expertise is distributed and a personal system cannot derive all specialist knowledge from one user, a networked PAI helps discover, evaluate, adopt and adapt methods developed by others. Practical refinements can be returned with authorization and validation, allowing individual experience to improve shared resources and validated contributions to increase the value available to others. Networks can also spread unreliable assumptions and unsafe practices, so privacy, provenance and validation are essential.
