# Networkability

## Sequence: **5/18**
- Utterance: Networkability extends personal intelligence through connections with people, agents, knowledge sources, shared expertise, and reusable workflows. Distributed expertise lets individuals adopt and adapt methods they did not develop, including specialised knowledge their own experiences cannot provide. Business workflows can be adapted to the user’s scale, objectives, approvals, budget, and tools. With appropriate authorisation and validation, selected practical refinements can be contributed back, creating a reciprocal exchange between shared expertise and individual experience. Networks can spread unreliable assumptions and unsafe practices. A trusted network needs clear sharing permissions and privacy, traceable provenance and attribution, and validation proportionate to intended use and consequences. Reusable methods should remain separate from private execution information such as supplier contracts, pricing, and internal priorities. Adopting a shared workflow should leave its requested access and actions subject to individual controls. Under these conditions, broader access to expertise and validated contributions can compound the value available to participants.
