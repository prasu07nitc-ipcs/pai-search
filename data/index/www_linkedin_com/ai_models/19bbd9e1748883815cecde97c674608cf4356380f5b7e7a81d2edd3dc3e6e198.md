# AI models

## Sequence: **6/18**
- Utterance: An AI model interprets information, reasons, synthesises knowledge, and generates possible actions. A model alone lacks the operational context, permissions, and mechanisms to pursue an individual’s goals in the digital world. In the PAI execution foundation, the model supplies cognitive capability, while the agent organises its use toward a goal and the harness supports and governs execution. These responsibilities can overlap within a product.
