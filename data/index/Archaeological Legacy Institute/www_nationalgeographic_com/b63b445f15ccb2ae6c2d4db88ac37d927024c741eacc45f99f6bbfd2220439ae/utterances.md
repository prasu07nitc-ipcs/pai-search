# Archaeological Legacy Institute

## Sequence: **5/27**
- Utterance: ALI partnered with Purdue University on a seven-day Nikumaroro expedition that disproved the proposed identification of the Taraia Object as Earhart's plane. Its specialists joined a 17-member team including archaeologists, Purdue representatives, Kiribati representatives and filmmakers. Executive director Rick Pettigrew persuaded Purdue to join despite TIGHAR's scepticism, later announced the object was debris rather than aircraft wreckage and said the hypothesis had been completely tested. He funded the $900,000 expedition using a repayable Purdue line of credit.
