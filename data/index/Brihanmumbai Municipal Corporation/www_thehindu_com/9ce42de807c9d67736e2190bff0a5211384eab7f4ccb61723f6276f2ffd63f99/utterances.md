# Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation

## Sequence: **3/15**
- Utterance: The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation, abbreviated BMC, appealed to citizens to avoid firecrackers and celebrate an environment-friendly Deepavali following a Bombay High Court directive amid rising city air pollution. It enlisted several celebrities, including Dia Mirza. On Monday, November 2, 2026, BMC selectively removed Mirza’s clip from social media after Mumbai BJP chief Ameet Satam’s objection and Mayor Ritu Tawde’s anti-Hindu comments about her; campaign videos featuring Madhuri Dixit, Nishigandha Wad and Ajinkya Rahane remained on BMC’s X and Instagram pages. On November 3, the High Court said BMC’s commissioner had been absolutely correct in selecting celebrities, criticised BMC’s deletion as an unwarranted knee-jerk reaction and directed it to re-upload Mirza’s video. The court recalled that BMC’s campaign followed its orders prohibiting firecrackers on public roads and streets throughout the year rather than at any particular festival.
