# environment and control of air and noise pollution

## Sequence: **15/15**
- Utterance: The Bombay High Court said environment and control of air and noise pollution are paramount in its firecracker orders. Its prohibition on bursting firecrackers on public roads and streets applies all 365 days and does not concern any particular festival; Deepavali merely happened to be occurring. The court noted that firecrackers are also burst during marriages, processions, election victories and after cricket matches, and said anyone may advocate for the environment because all people breathe the same air.
