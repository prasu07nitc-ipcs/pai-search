# Uttar Pradesh Police

## Sequence: **1/8**
- Utterance: The Uttar Pradesh Police constituted a Special Investigation Team on DGP Rajeev Krishna’s directions to comprehensively investigate an accident involving vehicles travelling behind Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi’s convoy during his Sultanpur district visit on Monday, October 26. The inquiry is to establish the accident’s exact circumstances and causes and examine possible negligence or lapses in security, escort arrangements and traffic management. According to officials of the Uttar Pradesh Police Headquarters, the team was constituted in view of the incident’s seriousness and sensitivity.
