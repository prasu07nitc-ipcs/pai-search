# Mahadevapura Assembly constituency

## Sequence: **3/16**
- Utterance: Karnataka’s Mahadevapura Assembly constituency was where the “vote chori (theft)” controversy first erupted. Following the SIR, The Hindu found 803 voter IDs linked to 397 people in this constituency, including some people with more than three IDs. Duplicate EPIC-number entries connected Mahadevapura with Dasarahalli, Sarvagnanagar and Hebbal; other cases involved multiple IDs within or across polling stations in Mahadevapura.
