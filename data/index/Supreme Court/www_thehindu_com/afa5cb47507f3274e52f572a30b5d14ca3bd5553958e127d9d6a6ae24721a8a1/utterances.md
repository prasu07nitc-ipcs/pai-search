# Supreme Court

## Sequence: **1/32**
- Utterance: On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the Supreme Court told Delhi traders that protests are not entirely hostile to business because a protest economy generates brisk earnings for enterprising hawkers and vendors at demonstration sites. A Bench headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant expressed sympathy for small traders, hawkers and push cart vendors whose right to livelihood competed with protestors’ right to expression, and said balance was necessary. The court was responding to Kirana Committee Delhi’s petition alleging losses during the two-month peak festive season around Dussehra and Deepavali. It issued notice on the plea, also supported by the New Delhi Traders Association, but refused an interim order without hearing protest organisers.
