# Supreme Court

## Sequence: **1/11**
- Utterance: On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the Supreme Court granted relief to Lok Sabha MP Abhishek Banerjee and asked the Calcutta High Court to consider his plea against media trial related to investigations and cases against him. A Bench comprising Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana said Banerjee could mention the matter for hearing when the High Court reopened. The Bench noted that the High Court was reopening on November 16 after the Durga Puja and Diwali break and said the petitioner was at liberty to mention the matter for consideration of interim relief. Senior advocate Shoeb Alam contended before the Bench that the Supreme Court had held in several judgments that media trials cannot take away the presumption of innocence.
