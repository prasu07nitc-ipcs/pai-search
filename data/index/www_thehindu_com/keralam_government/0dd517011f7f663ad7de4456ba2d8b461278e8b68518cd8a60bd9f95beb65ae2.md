# Keralam government

## Sequence: **1/9**
- Utterance: The Keralam government began checking ethanol levels at fuel outlets after sample testing in Karnataka found that petrol sold by several major oil companies contained ethanol far above the prescribed 20% level. The government decided to proceed after considering the Karnataka findings and consumer complaints and concerns about possible vehicle damage from contaminated fuel. Under a 2014 state government notification, the Commissioner of Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs and Taluk Supply Officers can inspect and initiate legal action.
