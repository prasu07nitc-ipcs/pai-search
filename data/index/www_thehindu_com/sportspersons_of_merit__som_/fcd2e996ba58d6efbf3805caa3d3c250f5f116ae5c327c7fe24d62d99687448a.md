# Sportspersons of Merit (SOM)

## Sequence: **2/20**
- Utterance: Eighty Sportspersons of Merit, 40 men and 40 women, formed the roll for electing the four women representatives. Four of six women candidates were elected on November 9, 2026. Four men were elected unopposed on November 7 after eight other candidates withdrew. The eight elected SOMs become members of the IOA General Assembly and can vote and stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls. One man and one woman among the SOMs will also join the Executive Council. The IOA Constitution requires a presidential candidate to be an SOM or a General Assembly member.
