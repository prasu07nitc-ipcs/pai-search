# Lok Sabha

## Sequence: **1/13**
- Utterance: The Lok Sabha had been adjourned sine die for 88 days without prorogation as of November 9, 2026. Congress communications chief Jairam Ramesh alleged the delay gave Union Home Minister Amit Shah time to secure a two-thirds majority in the Lok Sabha for the Delimitation Bills. Ramesh said Practice and Procedure of Parliament gives two to four days as the usual interval and cites a 28-day gap in the 2015 monsoon session. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju cited an 87-day gap in the 2008 monsoon session, which Ramesh said was agreed by all parties in light of the circumstances.
