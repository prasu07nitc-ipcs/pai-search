# Sheikh Hasina

## Sequence: **1/22**
- Utterance: Deposed Bangladeshi Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina requested a fresh passport to return from exile. Her passport was cancelled soon after she escaped to India in August 2024 following an uprising that toppled her government. At a press conference at the Foreign Correspondents Club on August 5, 2026, she said she planned to return to Bangladesh in December. India-based Awami League leaders repeatedly said she would follow through, and party sources said she might return via a land port with India, including Benapole-Petrapole. She has lived in New Delhi since being overthrown in the July–August 2024 student-people uprising and escaped on August 5, 2024. The International Crimes Tribunal sentenced her to death, and the verdict was later criticized because neither she nor her legal team was represented. She has refused to apologize for the police crackdown. In a written interview with The Hindu earlier in 2026, she questioned how police in the field could contact the prime minister for every action and how she could personally order officers in those circumstances. She said police operate under standard operating procedures, are trained for emergencies, crowd control and violent situations, and responded according to the situation when police personnel were attacked and killed and police stations were set on fire.
