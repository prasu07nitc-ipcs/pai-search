# Sheikh Hasina

## Sequence: **1/15**
- Utterance: Sheikh Hasina, the deposed prime minister of Bangladesh, seeks a new passport to return from exile. Her legal team sought a passport for her hours before Bangladesh announced its position on November 9, 2026. A source in Dhaka told The Hindu that Hasina requested a new passport and National Identity Card from Bangladesh’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs through a London-based legal firm. Her passport was revoked soon after she fled to India in August 2024 following an uprising that toppled her government. Acting Foreign Minister Humayun Kabir called Hasina a terrorist and said she fled after committing crimes, had been convicted and sentenced to death, and was responsible for violent crimes and financial fraud along with several Awami League colleagues. Bangladesh High Commission Press Secretary Md. Saidur Rahman said the authorities could not verify whether the passport request was made on Hasina’s behalf. Some Awami League leaders said on social media that preparations were underway for her return.
