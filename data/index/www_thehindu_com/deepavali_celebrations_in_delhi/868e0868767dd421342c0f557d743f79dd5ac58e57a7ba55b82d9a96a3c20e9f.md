# Deepavali celebrations in Delhi

## Sequence: **2/26**
- Utterance: Firecrackers were burst across Delhi on November 8, 2026, despite restrictions. Bursting picked up around 5–6 p.m. in areas including Pitampura, Burari, Mayur Vihar and parts of Lutyens’ Delhi. Smoke accumulated over roads and residential neighbourhoods as celebrations continued. Residents in several areas reported a strong smell of burnt crackers, reduced visibility and difficulty breathing as fireworks continued through the night. People burst crackers well past the Supreme Court’s 10 p.m. deadline.
