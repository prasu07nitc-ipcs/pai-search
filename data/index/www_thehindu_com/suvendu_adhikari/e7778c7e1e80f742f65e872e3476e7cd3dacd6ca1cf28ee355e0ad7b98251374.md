# Suvendu Adhikari

## Sequence: **19/20**
- Utterance: On November 3, West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari warned that criminals who harm women in the state would not be spared and pointed to what happens when someone attacks women. On November 9, he defended the killing of a rape-murder accused in a police encounter in Uttar Dinajpur, saying officers can fire in self-defence. Speaking to reporters in Digha in Purba Medinipur, Adhikari, who also holds the police portfolio, said police do not shoot every accused person, can retaliate when attacked and were authorised to act without clearance from superiors in cases involving rape and murder, drug trafficking and infiltration from Bangladesh. He said his government gave police a free hand in those cases and had zero tolerance for those offences.
