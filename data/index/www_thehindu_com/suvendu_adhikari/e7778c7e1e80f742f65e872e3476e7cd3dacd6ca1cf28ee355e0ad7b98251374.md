# Suvendu Adhikari

## Sequence: **12/13**
- Utterance: West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari, who also holds the police portfolio, warned on November 3 that criminals would not be spared for harming women in the state. On November 9, speaking to reporters in Digha, Purba Medinipur, he defended the encounter killing of a rape and murder accused in Uttar Dinajpur and said police could retaliate in self-defence if attacked. He said officers did not shoot every accused and that the government had authorised police to act without waiting for superiors’ clearance in cases involving rape and murder, drug trafficking, and infiltration from Bangladesh. He said the government had zero tolerance for those offences.
