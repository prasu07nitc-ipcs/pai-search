# William Belknap

## Sequence: **4/22**
- Utterance: William Belknap held the title of secretary of war in 1876 and became the first and, as of the report, only civilian leader of the U.S. military to be impeached by Congress. Accused of taking large kickbacks, he gave President Ulysses Grant his resignation before the scheduled impeachment vote and, according to a Senate history, burst into tears. The House impeached him anyway. The Senate later acquitted him after failing to reach the required two-thirds majority.
