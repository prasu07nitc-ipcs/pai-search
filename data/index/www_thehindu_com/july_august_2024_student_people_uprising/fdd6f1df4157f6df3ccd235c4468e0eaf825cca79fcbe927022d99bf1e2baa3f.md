# July–August 2024 student-people uprising

## Sequence: **7/22**
- Utterance: The student-people uprising in Bangladesh began in opposition to abolishing a quota system that ensured employment for families of guerrillas of the 1971 Liberation War. A police crackdown in mid-July 2024 killed hundreds of students and other citizens. The movement escalated into a protest against Sheikh Hasina, toppled her government and ultimately led to her escape on August 5, 2024.
