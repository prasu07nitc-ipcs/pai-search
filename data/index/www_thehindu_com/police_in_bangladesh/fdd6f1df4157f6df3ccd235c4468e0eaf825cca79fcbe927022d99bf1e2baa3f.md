# police in Bangladesh

## Sequence: **18/22**
- Utterance: A police crackdown in mid-July 2024 killed hundreds of students and other citizens, after which the movement escalated into a protest against Sheikh Hasina. Hasina refused to apologize for the crackdown. In a written interview with The Hindu, she said police in the field operate under standard operating procedures, are trained to handle emergencies, crowd control and violent situations, and responded according to the situation when police personnel were attacked and killed and police stations were set on fire. She questioned how officers could contact the prime minister for every action and how she could personally order them in those circumstances.
