# Practice and Procedure of Parliament

## Sequence: **8/13**
- Utterance: The eighth edition of Practice and Procedure of Parliament, popularly known as Kaul and Shakdher after its authors, was published in mid-2024. Jairam Ramesh said page 192 gives a usual interval of two to four days between Lok Sabha adjournment sine die and prorogation and that a footnote gives 28 days during the 2015 monsoon session as the longest gap. Ramesh said the book did not mention the 87-day gap in the 2008 monsoon session cited by Kiren Rijiju, an omission he found puzzling.
