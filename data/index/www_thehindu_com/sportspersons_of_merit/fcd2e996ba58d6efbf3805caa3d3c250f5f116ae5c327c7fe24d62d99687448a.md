# Sportspersons of Merit

## Sequence: **2/22**
- Utterance: On November 9, 2026, four women won Sportsperson of Merit seats from a field of six, including Saina Nehwal, Anju Bobby George, Dola Banerjee and Rani Rampal, while P. T. Usha lost. Eighty Sportspersons of Merit, 40 men and 40 women, form the electoral roll for the four female representatives. On November 7, Gagan Narang, Vijender Singh, Yogeshwar Dutt and Kamlesh Mehta won the four men's seats unopposed after eight other candidates withdrew. All eight elected SOMs will join the Indian Olympic Association General Assembly and can vote and stand in the December 5 Executive Council election. One man and one woman among the SOMs will also join the Executive Council.
