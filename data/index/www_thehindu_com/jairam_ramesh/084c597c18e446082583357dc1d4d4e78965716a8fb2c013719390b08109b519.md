## Sequence: **6/13**
- Utterance: On November 9, 2026, Congress leader and communications chief Jairam Ramesh alleged that the Lok Sabha had remained without prorogation for 88 days after adjournment sine die to give Union Home Minister Amit Shah time to muster a two-thirds majority for the Delimitation Bills. On X he cited page 192 of the eighth edition of Practice and Procedure of Parliament, published in mid-2024, for a usual interval of two to four days and a footnote citing a longest gap of 28 days in the 2015 monsoon session. He said the 88-day gap was a new record even on Rijiju’s reckoning. He alleged that, unlike previous gaps including 2008, the current delay was meant to help Shah manipulate a majority for bills he described as dangerous and destructive. He said Opposition unity and solidarity had defeated the government’s plans on April 17, 2026 and would prevail again if Shah used what Ramesh called his bulldozer tactics. He said Kharge had written to Narendra Modi four times in six months requesting a transparent, detailed all-party meeting before any delimitation legislation was introduced, and that Modi had repeatedly refused.
