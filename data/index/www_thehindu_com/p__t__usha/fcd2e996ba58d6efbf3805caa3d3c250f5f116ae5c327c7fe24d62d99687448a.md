# P. T. Usha

## Sequence: **1/22**
- Utterance: Indian Olympic Association President and track and field legend P. T. Usha failed to win election as one of four female Sportspersons of Merit on November 9, 2026, in a field of six. She can stand for the December 5 Executive Council elections only if a National Sports Federation nominates her to the General Assembly, which is considered unlikely. She is not expected to be one of the two Athletics Federation of India representatives. In 2022, she was elected unopposed as the IOA’s first woman president via the Sportspersons of Merit route. She complained to Electoral Officer Sanjay Kumar Srivasatava that former archer Dola Banerjee had violated the Model Code of Conduct. Usha objected to a WhatsApp message circulated through an account bearing Banerjee’s name that solicited support for the New Bharat Panel and asked voters to elect four named candidates, providing their ballot serial and roll numbers.
