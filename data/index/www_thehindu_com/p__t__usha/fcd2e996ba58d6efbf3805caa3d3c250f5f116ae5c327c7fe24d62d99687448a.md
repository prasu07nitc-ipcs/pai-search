# P. T. Usha

## Sequence: **1/22**
- Utterance: P. T. Usha, Indian Olympic Association president and a track and field legend, failed to win election as a Sportsperson of Merit on November 9, 2026, in a contested field of six women. She can stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls only if a National Sports Federation nominates her to the General Assembly. The article says that is unlikely and she is not expected to be one of the Athletics Federation of India's two representatives. Presidential candidates must be Sportspersons of Merit or General Assembly members. In 2022, Usha became the IOA's first woman president, elected unopposed via the SOM route. She complained to Electoral Officer Sanjay Kumar Srivasatava that former archer Dola Banerjee had violated the Model Code of Conduct by circulating a WhatsApp message soliciting votes. Usha said an account bearing Banerjee's name circulated the message seeking support for the New Bharat Panel and asking voters to elect four named candidates, listing their ballot serial and roll numbers. Sathish Sivalingam separately alleged an organized effort benefiting a candidate who was also IOA president, without naming Usha.
