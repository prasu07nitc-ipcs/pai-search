# U.S. military leadership reductions

## Sequence: **15/19**
- Utterance: Nearly two dozen generals, admirals and other military leaders had been fired, forced to retire or otherwise departed under Pete Hegseth, including the civilian Army and Navy secretaries and some top uniformed officers. Almost all departures were publicly unexplained by the Pentagon. Hegseth said he had ordered the military to double the overall top-officer cuts to a 20% decrease by January 1, 2027. The military had roughly 800 flag and general officer positions. Hegseth characterised the cuts as overdue accountability, while some members of Congress called them a purge that would take years to rebuild.
