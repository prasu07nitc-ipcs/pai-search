# P.T. Usha

## Sequence: **1/9**
- Utterance: Indian Olympic Association president P.T. Usha lodged a complaint with the Electoral Officer alleging that former archer Dola Banerjee violated the Model Code of Conduct by circulating a WhatsApp message soliciting votes for four candidates in the November 9, 2026 Sportspersons of Outstanding Merit elections. Usha objected to a message circulated through an account bearing Banerjee’s name that sought support for the New Bharat Panel and listed four candidates with their ballot serial and roll numbers. She cited Clause 1.9 of the code issued on October 30, 2026, arguing that the message amounted to targeted solicitation of votes through social media and appeared to fall within the clause. Her complaint also cited Clauses 1.2 and 1.3 on fair and respectful campaigning. She urged the election authorities to examine the communication, establish how it was created and circulated, and seek an explanation from the people concerned. She sought action under applicable election rules if a violation was established, directions to ensure a level playing field, and urgent intervention and a decision before voting began at 9 a.m. on November 9. A screenshot and a copy of the code accompanied her complaint.
