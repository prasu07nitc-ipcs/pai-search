# D. K. Shivakumar

## Sequence: **3/12**
- Utterance: Karnataka Chief Minister D. K. Shivakumar told reporters in Bengaluru on Sunday, November 8, 2026 that petrol samples were collected and tested at 67 sites after complaints about increased ethanol blending and engine performance. He said his government took the reported 31.5% blend seriously, issued instructions to the minister concerned and sent information to the Central Government. He said thousands of motorists reported reduced mileage, failure to cover expected distances and engines stalling in motion. He said inspections covered petrol pumps as well as blending and distribution, and records on where and how blending occurred were collected. He said the minister would hold an official Monday press conference on the inspection report and next steps. He held the Centre responsible for addressing the issue, prioritizing vehicle performance, fuel quality and public safety. He opposed increasing the ratio for profit from cheaper ethanol and said public inconvenience was unacceptable. He questioned the silence of BJP and JD(S) leaders and opposition leader R Ashoka and urged everyone to speak up on matters of public interest.
