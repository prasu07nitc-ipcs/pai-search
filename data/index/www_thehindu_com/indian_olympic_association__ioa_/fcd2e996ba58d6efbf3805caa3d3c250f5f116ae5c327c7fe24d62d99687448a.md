# Indian Olympic Association (IOA)

## Sequence: **5/20**
- Utterance: The Indian Olympic Association held Sportsperson of Merit elections as part of selecting its 2026–30 leadership. Its president P. T. Usha failed to win an SOM seat in the contested women’s election. The IOA Constitution requires presidential candidates to be an SOM or a General Assembly member. The eight elected SOMs enter the General Assembly and can vote and stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls. One man and one woman among the SOMs also join the Executive Council. Sathish Sivalingam alleged in a complaint that IOA resources were being used to influence voters in favour of a candidate who was also IOA president, without naming Usha.
