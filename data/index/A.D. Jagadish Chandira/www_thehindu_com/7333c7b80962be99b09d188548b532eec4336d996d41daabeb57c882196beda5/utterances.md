# A.D. Jagadish Chandira

## Sequence: **6/17**
- Utterance: Justice A.D. Jagadish Chandira sat with Justice B. Karthikeyan on the Madras High Court Division Bench that issued interim medical examination and treatment orders on November 3 in K. Arumugathammal's writ petition. During Tuesday's hearing, Chandira noted that the Tamil Nadu State Legal Services Authority had on Monday directed district legal services authority chairpersons to visit the prisons holding the 17 recaptured prisoners and inquire with them. The Bench therefore directed the medical team to accompany the Salem district authority chairperson to examine the petitioner's sons and two other recaptured remand prisoners.
