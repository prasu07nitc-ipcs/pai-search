# Rick Pettigrew

## Sequence: **14/27**
- Utterance: ALI executive director Rick Pettigrew persuaded Purdue University to join the Taraia Object expedition although TIGHAR leadership doubted the satellite image justified investigation. After remote sensing found no metal and further investigation ruled out Earhart's wreckage, he announced that the object was debris and said the expedition fully accomplished its goal by testing the hypothesis. He financed the $900,000 expedition through a Purdue line of credit that must be repaid.
