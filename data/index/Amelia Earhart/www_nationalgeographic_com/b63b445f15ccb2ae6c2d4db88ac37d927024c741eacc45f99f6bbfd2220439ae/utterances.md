# Amelia Earhart

## Sequence: **2/27**
- Utterance: Earhart and navigator Fred Noonan disappeared during the third-to-last leg of a round-the-world flight attempt in July 1937 after failing to find Howland Island. She radioed the Coast Guard cutter Itasca that they could not see it and fuel was low; her final confirmed message reported flying on line 157–337, north and south. Some researchers hypothesised they followed that line southeast to Nikumaroro, landed on its reef and survived as castaways, but the Taraia Object has now been ruled out as her aircraft. Other purported discoveries also failed: Deep Sea Vision's 2024 aircraft-like target was rock, a 2017 documentary's alleged capture photograph predated Earhart meeting Noonan, and Robert Ballard's 2019 expedition did not find the plane. Nauticos is fundraising for a fourth search based on a fuel-exhaustion ocean-crash theory and radio experiments.
