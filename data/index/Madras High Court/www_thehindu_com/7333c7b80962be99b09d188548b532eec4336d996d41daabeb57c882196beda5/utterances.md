# Madras High Court

## Sequence: **5/17**
- Utterance: The Madras High Court received K. Arumugathammal's writ petition concerning alleged ill-treatment and assault of recaptured prisoners. On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, a Division Bench of Justices A.D. Jagadish Chandira and B. Karthikeyan issued interim orders for medical examination of her sons and two specifically named inmates. After learning all four were at Salem Central Prison, the Bench ordered three government doctors to visit with the district legal services authority chairperson on Wednesday, November 4, examine the prisoners, provide necessary treatment, and submit a report by November 6, the next hearing date. It directed the medical team to accompany the Salem district legal services authority chairperson.
