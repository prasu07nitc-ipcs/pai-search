# Form 6

## Sequence: **16/21**
- Utterance: The ECI denied allegations that Form 6 was amended in SIR orders dated June 24, 2025, October 27, 2025 and May 14, 2026. It described Form 6 as the statutory form for inclusion in electoral rolls and distinguished the additional mapping declaration that applicants submit with it during SIR. According to the ECI, mapping helps voters avoid further verification documents and does not change Form 6. The ECI said 88 of 97 initially excluded Goa voters had submitted Form 6 and their applications had been accepted; six others were abroad and three held Portuguese passports.
