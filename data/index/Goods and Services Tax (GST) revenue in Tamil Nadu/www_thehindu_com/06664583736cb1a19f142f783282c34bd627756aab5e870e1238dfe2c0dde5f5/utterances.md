# Goods and Services Tax (GST) revenue in Tamil Nadu

## Sequence: **2/6**
- Utterance: Opposition parties said Tamil Nadu’s GST revenue growth rate had fallen below the national average under the TVK government. Minister for Commercial Taxes and Registration D. Logesh Tamilselvan responded on November 3, 2026, that gross GST collection figures alone could not measure the State’s economic performance. In a post on X, he explained that gross GST collection includes SGST, the Union government’s CGST and IGST, but not all IGST collected belongs to Tamil Nadu because it includes amounts attributable to other States. He said Tamil Nadu’s actual GST revenue comprises SGST collections and its apportioned IGST settlement. He argued that because September 2025 GST rate rationalisation reduced tax rates on several commodities, a decline in gross GST collections alone could not assess economic performance. He said Tamil Nadu’s actual GST revenue grew 15% up to October in the current financial year and 8% in October alone.
