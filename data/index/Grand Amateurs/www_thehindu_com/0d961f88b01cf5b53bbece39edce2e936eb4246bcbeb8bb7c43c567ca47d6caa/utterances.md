# Grand Amateurs

## Sequence: **12/23**
- Utterance: William Lassell, William Rutter Dawes and James Nasmyth were recognised as the Grand Amateurs of astronomy. Together they built a 24-inch telescope at Starfield. Its 20-foot-long tube had a two-foot-wide mirror polished with a machine designed by Lassell. It was the first telescope of its size to achieve equatorial mounting, enabling specific objects to be tracked and observed for longer periods.
