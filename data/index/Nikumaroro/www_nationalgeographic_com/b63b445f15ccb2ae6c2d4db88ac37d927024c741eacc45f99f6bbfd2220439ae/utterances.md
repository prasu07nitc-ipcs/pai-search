# Nikumaroro

## Sequence: **3/27**
- Utterance: Nikumaroro is a remote Pacific atoll belonging to Kiribati, formerly called Gardner Island, approximately 400 miles from Earhart's intended destination of Howland Island. It hosts enormous omnivorous coconut crabs. A British colony existed there between 1938 and 1963; former residents recalled aircraft wreckage in the lagoon and reef, and aircraft-material handicrafts were found in the abandoned village. Bones discovered in a clearing in 1940 were taken to Fiji and later disappeared; modern analysis of a colonial doctor's measurements indicated a woman of European descent. Later excavations found campfire remains, a jackknife, 1930s cosmetic-bottle fragments and a compact mirror retaining rouge. Forensic dogs in 2017 indicated former human remains, but excavation found none. A recent Purdue/ALI team investigated and ruled out the lagoon's Taraia Object as Earhart's plane.
