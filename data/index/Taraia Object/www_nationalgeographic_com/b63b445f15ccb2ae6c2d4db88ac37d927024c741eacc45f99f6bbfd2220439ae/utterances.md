# Taraia Object

## Sequence: **1/27**
- Utterance: The vaguely plane-shaped feature in Nikumaroro's lagoon was named for a nearby peninsula and first spotted by an amateur researcher in 2020 in satellite imagery from 2015. Later imagery obscured it. Expedition members initially considered it very likely to be Amelia Earhart's Lockheed Electra 10-E, while sceptics suggested a palm or pandanus tree and root ball, and TIGHAR leadership doubted the image justified an expedition. A Purdue/ALI expedition located an object despite its fading visibility, but remote sensing detected no metal and subsequent investigation confirmed it was not Earhart's aircraft wreckage. Rick Pettigrew described it as a collection of debris; Purdue attributed supposed metallic reflections in photographs dating to 1938 to a shiny concretized amalgam of coral sand.
