# Tasmac

## Sequence: **2/6**
- Utterance: Many Tasmac shops closed on Sunday without informing management. Tasmac had announced the previous week that the maximum retail price of all liquor variants in the State would increase by ₹10 from November 1 through an environment cess added to existing prices, reimbursable to tipplers returning empty liquor bottles. Tasmac, described as the cash cow of the Tamil Nadu government, witnesses its highest sales during Deepavali, Pongal and New Year. Tasmac suspended its workers across Tamil Nadu responsible for shop closures on Sunday and Monday. As of July 2026, Tasmac had 4,048 retail vending shops and 22,877 workers: 6,473 supervisors, 14,379 salespersons and 2,025 assistant salespersons. Its revenue for 2025–2026 was ₹50,845 crore. These claims were published on November 03, 2026 at 05:00 am IST.
