# eco-friendly, firecracker-free Deepavali campaign

## Sequence: **4/15**
- Utterance: BMC’s eco-friendly Deepavali campaign appealed to citizens to avoid bursting firecrackers amid rising air pollution, following a Bombay High Court directive. It featured several celebrities, including Dia Mirza, whose clip urged people to avoid noise and air pollution. BMC removed her video on November 2, 2026, following political objections, while videos featuring Madhuri Dixit, Nishigandha Wad and Ajinkya Rahane remained on its X and Instagram pages. On November 3 the High Court directed restoration of Mirza’s clip, stressed that its message benefited everyone regardless of religion or caste and recalled that the underlying firecracker prohibition on public roads and streets applies throughout the year, not to a particular festival.
