# Purvanchal Expressway convoy collision

## Sequence: **2/11**
- Utterance: According to a police officer, some vehicles in Rahul Gandhi’s convoy collided around 10:15am on Monday, October 26, when a private vehicle at the front, belonging to local Congress leaders, suddenly braked for an unspecified reason and vehicles behind it crashed into one another. Police said the affected vehicles had joined midway carrying passengers including Congress workers from neighbouring Amethi district. The officer said the incident was not serious, no major injuries were reported, Gandhi was safe, and the convoy soon continued to Sultanpur. Anshu Awasthi said Gandhi’s vehicle was not involved and described a minor brush.
