# James Nasmyth

## Sequence: **11/23**
- Utterance: Scottish engineer James Nasmyth invented the steam hammer and turned to astronomy after retiring from business as a mechanical engineer. He partnered with William Lassell and William Rutter Dawes; known together as the Grand Amateurs of astronomy, they built a 24-inch telescope in Lassell’s Starfield workshops.
