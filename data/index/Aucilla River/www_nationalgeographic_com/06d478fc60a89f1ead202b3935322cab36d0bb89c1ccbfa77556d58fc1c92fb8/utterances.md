# Aucilla River

## Sequence: **20/27**
- Utterance: The Aucilla River, around an hour south of Tallahassee, is a productive underwater archaeological location with dark water, alligators and water moccasins. In 2016 researchers found a stone knife and mastodon bones bearing signs of human butchery in a sinkhole 30 feet below the surface, dating to around 14,500 years ago and helping establish earlier North American settlement. In 1981 amateur divers in a nearby tributary found an extinct bison's skull with a broken spear point in its forehead, apparently from a hunter's lethal strike 10,500 years earlier. Following the Aucilla and nearby rivers offshore led to approximately 25 additional excavated sites.
