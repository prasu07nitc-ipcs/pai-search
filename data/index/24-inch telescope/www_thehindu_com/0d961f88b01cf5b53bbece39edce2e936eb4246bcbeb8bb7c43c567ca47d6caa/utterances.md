# 24-inch telescope

## Sequence: **13/23**
- Utterance: William Lassell, William Rutter Dawes and James Nasmyth built a 24-inch telescope in Lassell’s Starfield workshops. Its tube was 20 feet long, and its two-foot-wide mirror was polished using a machine of Lassell’s design. It was the first telescope of its size to achieve equatorial mounting, enabling specific objects to be tracked and observed for longer. With it, Lassell discovered Triton in 1846, Hyperion in 1848, and Ariel and Umbriel in 1851.
