# police investigating the illicit liquor truck incident

## Sequence: **1/13**
- Utterance: Officials said on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, that police had expanded their investigation across Madhya Pradesh and neighbouring Gujarat after a policeman was killed the previous day while trying to intercept an illicit liquor-laden truck. Police arrested three persons, including the alleged kingpin of a bootlegging ring, and sent a team to Gujarat, where absconding driver Gajendra alias Akku Rawat was suspected to be hiding. They arrested Rahul Jaiswal, Keram Singh Changod and Vijay Rao and announced a ₹10,000 reward for the arrest of each absconding accused. Dewas Superintendent of Police Aditya Mishra said efforts were being made to gather as much scientific evidence as possible in the death of the police station in-charge crushed by a truck carrying over 1,800 litres of illicit liquor. Officials said police teams were searching for Rawat in Alirajpur, Dhar, Jhabua, Dewas and Indore districts, where he had previously lived, gathering information about his relatives and contacts, and also searching in Gujarat on suspicion that he was hiding there. Police accused Jaiswal of operating an illegal liquor smuggling network from Timarni in Harda district to Gujarat, said Keram Singh provided Rawat with the truck for smuggling, and said Rao had reportedly admitted working for Jaiswal.
