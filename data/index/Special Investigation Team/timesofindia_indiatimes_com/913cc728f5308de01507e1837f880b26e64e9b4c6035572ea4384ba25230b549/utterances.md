# Special Investigation Team

## Sequence: **2/8**
- Utterance: A three-member Special Investigation Team was constituted by the Uttar Pradesh Police on DGP Rajeev Krishna’s directions to comprehensively probe the collision involving vehicles travelling behind Rahul Gandhi’s convoy during his Sultanpur district visit on Monday, October 26. Inspector General of Police, Lucknow Range, Kiran S will head the team; its other members are Sultanpur Additional Superintendent of Police Brij Narayan Singh and Sultanpur Deputy Superintendent of Police (Traffic) RK Chaturvedi. The team combines senior police leadership, district-level investigation expertise and traffic policing experience. It must examine the accident’s causes, circumstances, sequence of events and contributing factors; assess possible negligence or lapses in security, escort management and traffic regulation, including whether prescribed arrangements were followed; and recommend measures to prevent similar accidents during future VIP convoy movements. Its findings are expected to identify potential gaps in coordination, escort deployment and traffic management, with corrective measures where necessary. Uttar Pradesh Police Headquarters directed it to investigate all aspects and submit a detailed report to headquarters as soon as possible. The source states that its findings will establish the sequence of events, determine whether lapses occurred and recommend improvements in convoy safety and traffic coordination.
