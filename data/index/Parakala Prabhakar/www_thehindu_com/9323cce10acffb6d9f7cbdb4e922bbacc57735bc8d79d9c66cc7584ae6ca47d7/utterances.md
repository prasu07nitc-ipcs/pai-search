# Parakala Prabhakar

## Sequence: **1/7**
- Utterance: Economist and political commentator Parakala Prabhakar called the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls an exercise to create two classes of Indians by disenfranchising several marginalised sections of the population. Responding to Union Home Minister Amit Shah’s comments declaring three crore deleted voters to be illegal infiltrators from Bangladesh, Prabhakar questioned whether the legislative bodies and elected governments following the 2014, 2019 and 2024 elections were unlawful. At a press meet organised by Telangana Voter Rights Forum on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, he challenged those concerned to resign and conduct elections after purification of the voters list if Shah’s claim was true. He observed that citizens marked permanently shifted must have moved somewhere, increasing voter numbers there, yet voter numbers were declining in all States and constituencies. Recalling a Supreme Court order of May 27 seeking submission within four months of a list of illegal immigrants identified in SIR, he said that even a small list had not been produced five months after the order. He said the Home Minister should have given State-wise numbers of illegal immigrants and accused him of bringing down the stature of his post by making flippant comments.
