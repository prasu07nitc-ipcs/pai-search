# Abhishek Banerjee

## Sequence: **2/11**
- Utterance: Lok Sabha MP Abhishek Banerjee received relief from the Supreme Court on November 3, 2026, when it asked the Calcutta High Court to consider his plea against media trial related to investigations and cases against him. The Supreme Court said he could mention the matter for hearing and consideration of interim relief when the High Court reopened on November 16 after the Durga Puja and Diwali break. His senior advocate Shoeb Alam said the High Court had heard the matter only once, when the petitioner was told it did not have the roster, and that Alam had subsequently mentioned it three times without a hearing while Banerjee’s rights continued to be violated. Banerjee sought a High Court order restraining investigating agencies from sharing his personal information with media outlets. He alleged that selective leaks by the investigating officer were tarnishing his image and could influence ongoing investigations against him. Under investigation in multiple cases, he alleged that probe agencies were leaking details of searches, interrogations and other legal steps to a section of the media and that several articles had appeared in different newspapers. He contended that deliberate leaks by police officials violated his rights to dignity, reputation and a fair investigation under Article 21 of the Constitution and could tarnish his public image and influence investigations against him. He also sought a direction restraining media outlets from reporting or publishing news about him concerning matters forming part of ongoing investigations.
