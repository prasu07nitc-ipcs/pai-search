# William Rutter Dawes

## Sequence: **10/23**
- Utterance: English amateur astronomer William Rutter Dawes had an observatory in Ormskirk and a keen eye for testing and calibrating telescopes. He partnered with William Lassell and James Nasmyth. Known together as the Grand Amateurs of astronomy, they built a 24-inch telescope in Lassell’s Starfield workshops.
