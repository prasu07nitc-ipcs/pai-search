# Nikumaroro expedition

## Sequence: **18/27**
- Utterance: The 17-member Purdue/ALI expedition included Purdue's general counsel, two astronaut alumni, two RECON Offshore maritime archaeologists, ALI specialists, Kiribati representatives and filmmakers. Departing October 7 and arriving October 13, they landed in blustery conditions, cleared a jungle path to the lagoon and installed weather and tide monitoring. On day two, magnetometer and sonar surveying located the fading Taraia Object but detected no metal. Rough seas blocked landing from day three through October 17; extending the mission enabled an October 18 return. Investigation disproved the aircraft hypothesis. The seven-day expedition cost $900,000, funded through Pettigrew's repayable Purdue credit line.
