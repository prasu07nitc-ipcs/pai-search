# Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls

## Sequence: **2/7**
- Utterance: The ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls was described by economist and political commentator Parakala Prabhakar as an exercise to create two classes of Indians by disenfranchising several marginalised sections of the population. He said a Supreme Court order of May 27 had sought a list of illegal immigrants identified in SIR within four months, but even a small list had not been produced five months after the order. Academic and activist G. Haragopal sought scrapping of the SIR process.
