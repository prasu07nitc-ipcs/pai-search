## Sequence: **4/32**
- Utterance: As Chief Justice of India heading the Bench, Surya Kant expressed sympathy for the livelihood rights of small traders, hawkers and push cart vendors and said these needed balancing with protestors’ expression rights. He said the rights to protest and trade are constitutionally recognised, explaining that he meant people trading as their sole livelihood, such as daily wagers and the subjiwala, rather than commercial establishments, and asked where they could go. He said everyone has a right to protest but must follow permission conditions, cannot occupy a protest site indefinitely, and must consider where other protestors would go.
