# Tushar Mehta

## Sequence: **13/32**
- Utterance: Solicitor General Tushar Mehta represented the Union government. He said Cockroach Janta Party-linked NEET-UG paper leak protestors had obtained permission for a peaceful one-day Jantar Mantar demonstration on June 20 but refused to leave, and that the demonstration culminated in violence after an unauthorised Sansad Chalo march to Parliament on July 20. He argued that protest or bandh organisers must accept responsibility for others’ losses. When Menaka Guruswamy alleged 7,000 illegal detentions during anti-CEC protests on October 10–11, he called them removals rather than detentions. Challenging the privacy argument concerning public protests, he raised women’s safety and said over 500 influencers were videographing protestors while protestors objected to government surveillance. He said the apex court had held legitimate state interest to be an exception to the right to privacy.
