# Gajendra alias Akku Rawat

## Sequence: **2/13**
- Utterance: Truck driver Gajendra alias Akku Rawat was absconding after Khategaon Station House Officer Dilip Puri was killed and a constable injured while trying to intercept a truck carrying 1,800 litres of illicit liquor on the Indore-Betul Highway on Monday, November 2, 2026. Puri had received information about illicit liquor smuggling and tried to question the driver of a truck parked near a roadside eatery; the driver allegedly drove over the road divider to escape, and the truck’s rear wheel ran over Puri. Officials said on November 3 that Rawat was suspected to be hiding in Gujarat. Police searched Alirajpur, Dhar, Jhabua, Dewas and Indore districts because he had previously lived there, gathered information about his relatives and contacts, and sent a team to Gujarat. According to police, Keram Singh supplied Rawat with the truck for smuggling.
