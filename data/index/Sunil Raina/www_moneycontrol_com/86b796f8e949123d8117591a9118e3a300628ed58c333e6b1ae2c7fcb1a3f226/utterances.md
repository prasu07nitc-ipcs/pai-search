# Sunil Raina

## Sequence: **2/17**
- Utterance: Sources told Moneycontrol that Sunil Raina has quit as Lava International’s managing director, is serving his notice period and will remain until December. They said he is leaving to explore business opportunities outside Lava. He became managing director in 2023 after co-founder and then managing director Hari Om Rai was arrested by the Enforcement Directorate in a money-laundering case. Raina joined Lava in 2010 as vice-president and chief marketing officer and subsequently held key leadership positions as it sought to rebuild its smartphone business. Speaking to Moneycontrol on the sidelines of the India Mobile Congress earlier this month, he said Lava’s smartphone business had grown 60-70 percent this year despite an overall market contraction, and that it focused on gaining market share from competitors rather than relying on industry growth.
