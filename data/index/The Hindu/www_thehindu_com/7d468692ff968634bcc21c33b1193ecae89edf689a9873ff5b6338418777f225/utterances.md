# The Hindu

## Sequence: **2/16**
- Utterance: The Hindu found that after the SIR, Karnataka’s Mahadevapura Assembly constituency alone had 803 voter IDs linked to 397 people; in several cases, more than three voter IDs had been issued under one person’s name. It found at least seven cases across constituencies in which the same EPIC number appeared against two entries: seven EPIC numbers accounted for 14 final-roll entries, with three cases each between Mahadevapura and Dasarahalli and between Mahadevapura and Sarvagnanagar, and one between Mahadevapura and Hebbal. In another set of cases, it found seven people with more than three voter IDs each. It also found more than 500 cases in Mahadevapura in which the same person had two or more voter IDs within one polling station, and 300 other cases in which the same person had multiple IDs across different polling stations in the constituency. The article was published on November 04, 2026 at 06:05 am IST.
