# Flávio Bolsonaro

## Sequence: **3/17**
- Utterance: Lula's challenger and Jair Bolsonaro's eldest son led the first round by about 1.8 percentage points or 2.2 million votes but did not reach 50%, prompting an October 25 runoff. He entered the runoff with momentum and told reporters in Brasília that Brazilians demanded change and that the Workers Party's era was ending. His supporters formed a majority of governors elected in the first round. Evangelical voters increasingly consolidated behind the Bolsonaro family; Flávio won 52% of valid votes in municipalities with above-average evangelical concentrations. His campaign was expected to emphasise anti-PT sentiment, seek supporters of eliminated right-wing candidates, and approach roughly 40 million abstaining, blank-ballot or invalid-ballot voters. He secured political support in five states with gubernatorial runoffs, compared with Lula's four.
