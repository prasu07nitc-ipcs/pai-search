# In Karnataka seat where ‘vote chori’ claims first surfaced, over 800 voter IDs linked to 397 names after SIR

## Description

Karnataka’s Mahadevapura shows 803 voter IDs linked to 397 people after SIR, with duplicates across constituencies and multiple IDs raised concerns over electoral integrity.
