# Searching the Seabed for Florida’s Ice Age Societies

## Description

Thousands of years ago, Florida’s coastline extended as much as 100 miles out beyond its present edge. Artifacts from that early era are now hidden underwater from Texas to Florida—and they could help settle major debates about when humans arrived in America.
