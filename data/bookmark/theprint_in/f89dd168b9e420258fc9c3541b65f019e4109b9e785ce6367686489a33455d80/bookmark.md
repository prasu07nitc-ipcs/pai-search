# Rahul Gandhi's convoy involved in minor collision on Purvanchal Expressway; Cong leader safe

## Description

Sultanpur (UP), Oct 26 (PTI) Some vehicles in Congress leader Rahul Gandhi’s convoy collided on the Purvanchal Expressway on Monday while he was on his way to Sultanpur for a court hearing in a defamation case against him, a police officer said.
