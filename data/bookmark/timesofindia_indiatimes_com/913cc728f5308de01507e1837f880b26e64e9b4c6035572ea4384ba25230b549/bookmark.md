# SIT formed to probe accident involving vehicles behind Rahul Gandhi’s convoy in Sultanpur

## Description

The Uttar Pradesh Police have constituted a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to conduct a comprehensive probe into an accident involving vehicles travelling behind the convoy of Leader of Opposition in the Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi during his visit to Sultanpur district on Monday.
